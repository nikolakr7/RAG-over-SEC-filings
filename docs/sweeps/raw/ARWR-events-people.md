## 8-K EVENTS (chronological)

1. **2026-01-06 (filed 2026-01-06)** — Items 7.01 / 8.01, clinical data. Interim Phase 1/2a results for the two wholly owned obesity RNAi candidates: ARO-INHBE (single 400 mg dose: mean -85% serum Activin E; week 16 monotherapy: visceral fat -9.9%, liver fat -38%; two doses at week 24: visceral fat -15.6% placebo-adjusted; combination with tirzepatide roughly doubled weight loss and tripled fat reduction vs tirzepatide alone in obese T2DM patients) and ARO-ALK7 (first RNAi therapeutic to show adipocyte gene silencing in the clinic; -88% mean adipose ALK7 mRNA at 200 mg, week 8; visceral fat -14.1% placebo-adjusted at week 8). ## CROSS-COMPANY PEOPLE LINKS

### CONFIRMED: Douglas Ingram = Sarepta's CEO on Arrowhead's board (ARWR-SRPT board interlock)

The 10-K flag ("Douglass Ingram") is confirmed, with a spelling wrinkle: the proxies consistently use **"Douglas Ingram"** (one s); the 10-K signature page spelled it "Douglass." He is a distinct person from **Douglass Given** (two s), ARWR's former Board Chairman who resigned 2024-12-31.

Exact bio sentences, 2025 proxy (lines 505-517):
> "Director since: New Nominee"
> "Other Public Company Boards: • Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc. • Relay Therapeutics, Inc."
> "Mr. Ingram has served as the President and Chief Executive Officer of Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: SRPT), a biopharmaceutical company for rare diseases, since June,2017. Prior to joining Sarepta, Mr. Ingram served as a Chief Executive Officer, President and Director of Chase Pharmaceuticals Corporation, a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company from December 2015 until November 2016. Prior to joining Chase Pharmaceuticals, Mr. Ingram served as the President of Allergan, Inc., a pharmaceutical company, from July 2013 until it was acquired by Actavis in early 2015."
> "Mr. Ingram's qualifications to serve on the Board include his deep understanding of the business through his role as President and Chief Executive Officer of Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc., which gives him an extensive understanding of our business and operations."

When he joined: 2025 proxy line 439: "Douglas Ingram is expected to be appointed to the board before our 2025 Annual Meeting and was initially recommended to the Board by our CEO and, other executive officers." 2026 proxy line 1077: "Mr. Ingram joined the Board during fiscal year 2025 and his cash compensation was pro-rated for such fiscal year." (fiscal 2025 = Oct 2024-Sep 2025; sign-on RSU grant valued at $786,350, vesting in three annual installments). So he joined within months of the November 25, 2024 Sarepta-Arrowhead $500M+ collaboration signing, while serving as Sarepta's CEO, and exited via retirement at the 2026-03-19 annual meeting (2026 proxy line 550: "Douglas Ingram, a current director, is not standing for re-election and will retire from the Board at the annual meeting...").

Timeline for the graph: Sarepta deal signed 2024-11-25 -> Ingram recommended by ARWR CEO/officers, appointed to ARWR board before the March 2025 annual meeting -> deal closed 2025-02-07 -> DM1 milestones triggered Jul and Nov 2025 -> Ingram retires from ARWR board 2026-03-19. (Note: Sarepta was also removed from ARWR's compensation peer group in fiscal 2025, 2026 proxy line 1869.)
