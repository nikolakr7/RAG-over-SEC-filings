## Q1 FY2025 — filed 2025-02-10 (qtr ended 12/31/2024)
- **Sarepta**: deal signed Nov 25, 2024 but NOT yet closed at quarter-end/filing — closing explicitly conditioned on HSR expiration/termination "and other customary conditions" (line 2444 area, same boilerplate reused in later filings). No $500M/$325M received yet.
- GSK-HBV $2.5M milestone (5th Phase 2 patient dosed) "paid in the second quarter of fiscal 2025" — i.e., this quarter (line 1882, 1997).
- **Ionis**: absent (predates Sept 2025 filings by 8 months).
- **REDEMPLO**: not yet approved/named; plozasiran still investigational.
- No other new deals this quarter beyond Sarepta signing.

## Q2 FY2025 — filed 2025-05-12 (qtr ended 3/31/2025)
- **Sarepta**: deal CLOSED Feb 7, 2025 (HSR cleared); $500M upfront + $325M equity investment received this quarter (lines 2444-2496). First quarter with Sarepta collaboration revenue recognized.
- **Ionis**: absent (litigation still 5 months away).
- **REDEMPLO**: not yet approved.
- No new deals beyond Sarepta close.

## Q3 FY2025 — filed 2025-08-07 (qtr ended 6/30/2025)
- **Sarepta milestone**: first DM1 $100M milestone TRIGGERED July 27, 2025 — reported as a Note 15 Subsequent Event (post-quarter-end; quarter itself ended 6/30/2025), tied to reaching the first of two enrollment targets + dose-escalation authorization in the ARO-DM1 Phase 1/2 study (line 4698-4699 area).
- **New deal — Sanofi/Visirna**: also a Subsequent Event (Note 15, lines 4686-4697): Aug 1, 2025 Asset Purchase Agreement, Visirna HK sells Greater-China plozasiran rights to Genzyme Corporation ("Sanofi"); deal "scheduled to close during the second half of the year" (i.e., not yet closed as of this filing) — $130M upfront + up to $265M milestones + royalties, once closed.
- **Ionis**: absent (litigation begins Sept 10-11, 2025, ~5 weeks after this filing).
- **REDEMPLO**: not yet approved (FDA approval Nov 18, 2025 postdates this filing).

## Q1 FY2026 — filed 2026-02-05 (qtr ended 12/31/2025)
- **Ionis litigation — full arrival**: Note 7 Litigation (lines 3289-3293) discloses both Sept 2025 complaints (Arrowhead's Sept 10 Delaware DJ action re: '333 patent; Ionis's Sept 11 C.D. Cal. infringement complaint) AND a new development: **"On December 23, 2025, the court granted Ionis' motion to dismiss"** — Arrowhead's own Delaware declaratory-judgment action was dismissed on Ionis's motion. The C.D. Cal. infringement suit continues ("Company disputes the allegations...intends to vigorously defend itself"). No contingent liability recorded.

- **New deal — Novartis**: "Entered into a global licensing and collaboration agreement with Novartis Pharma AG...on August 29, 2025, which closed on October 17, 2025" (line 2416) — now closed and generating revenue this quarter; disclosed alongside Sarepta as the two active collaboration/license revenue sources (line 2513: "as of December 31, 2025, relate solely to the Company's agreements with Sarepta and Novartis").
- **Sarepta milestone**: second DM1 milestone ($200M) "triggered on November 20, 2025" (line 2408), when the second of two enrollment targets was reached — reported as occurring within this quarter.
- Sanofi/Visirna deal now presumably closed (Novartis + Sarepta cited as the only revenue-generating agreements as of 12/31/2025 per line 2513 — Sanofi not listed yet, suggesting its close/revenue recognition trails into a later quarter).
