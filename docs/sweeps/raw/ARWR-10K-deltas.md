# ARWR 10-K deltas: FY2024 (filed 2024-11-26) and FY2023 (filed 2023-11-29) vs FY2025 baseline

Sources: `data/text/ARWR/10-K_2024-11-26_000162828024049277.txt` ("FY2024"); `data/text/ARWR/10-K_2023-11-29_000162828023040219.txt` ("FY2023"). Neither has an EX-21 exhibit file; subsidiaries only from prose. Baseline = `ARWR-10K-FY2025.md`.

## 1. Deals

- **Sarepta**: FY2024 10-K reports the deal as brand-new, signed one day before filing. Lines 3289-3291, 7230-7238: "On November 25, 2024, the Company entered into an Exclusive License and Collaboration Agreement (the "Collaboration Agreement") with Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc." — worldwide exclusive license to ARO-DUX4/ARO-DM1/ARO-MMP7/ARO-ATXN2 (clinical) + sublicensable license to ARO-HTT/ARO-ATXN1/ARO-ATXN3 (preclinical) + up to 6 discovery targets. Same-day Stock Purchase Agreement (private placement) with a Sarepta affiliate (line 3682, 3453 exhibit refs). Deal had NOT yet closed at filing (closing was Feb 7, 2025 per baseline) — no $500M upfront, no revenue recognized, no 17-performance-obligation accounting yet; this is purely subsequent-events/signing language, not a Note-2 collaboration with recognized revenue. FY2023 10-K: zero mentions of Sarepta anywhere (pre-deal state confirmed).
- **Amgen/olpasiran → Royalty Pharma**: materially IDENTICAL description in both older filings and baseline — Nov 9, 2022 Royalty Purchase Agreement, Royalty Pharma entitled to all Amgen royalties on olpasiran, Arrowhead keeps milestones. FY2024 adds one fact not in FY2023: the $50M OCEAN(a) enrollment-completion milestone was actually PAID in Q3 FY2024 (line 2870, 6964-6967) — in FY2023 that same milestone is still prospective/unpaid ("remains eligible to receive up to an additional $... million," line 5153).
- **Janssen HBV return + GSK relicense (Dec 2023)**: FY2023 has it freshest/most granular — dated Oct 30, 2023 Assignment and Consent Agreement where Arrowhead "consented to the assignment of the Janssen License Agreement to GSK, which assignment shall be effective upon the receipt of certain anti-trust approvals" (line 2671, 6783) — i.e., FY2023 (filed Nov 29, 2023) captures the deal as agreed-but-not-yet-closed/in transition. Separately, FY2023 also states Apr 7, 2023 Janssen voluntarily terminated the ARO-PNPLA3 collaboration, rights returned to Arrowhead (line 1520, 5131). FY2024 has the completed picture: "On December 11, 2023, GSK and the Company entered into the GSK-HBV Agreement" (line 2957) with $2.7M received FY2024 upon signing (line 2960) — confirms close occurred between the two filings, consistent with baseline's "Dec 11, 2023" GSK-HBV Agreement date.
- **Visirna/Cayman VIE**: substantively unchanged prose across FY2023, FY2024, and baseline — Apr 25, 2022 Visirna License Agreement; VIE consolidation reasoning identical boilerplate (lines 4831-4834 FY2023; 5178-5181 FY2024). FY2023 adds formation color absent from FY2024/baseline: "$65.2 million was primarily related to the formation of the Company's joint venture, Visirna" (line 3139) and a $[X] million upfront capital contribution to Visirna (line 5159) — earliest-stated JV funding detail. FY2024 adds the Visirna ESOP: "On October 1, 2023, Visirna...granted [X] stock options...from the...Visirna ESOP" (line 6227) — not present in FY2023 (ESOP postdates FY2023 fiscal year-end of Sept 30, 2023, consistent with an Oct 1, 2023 grant date).
- **Horizon/HZN-457 termination**: FY2023 captures it in real time — "On November 21, 2023, the Company received notice from Horizon that it has elected to terminate the Horizon License Agreement...effective on December 21, 2023" (line 1490, 5059), i.e. filed Nov 29, 2023, eight days after notice and before the effective termination date. FY2024 has it as settled history: "On October 6, 2023, Amgen completed its acquisition of Horizon and subsequently notified the Company of Amgen's intent to terminate...took effect on December 21, 2023" (line 5316).

## 2. Subsidiary / structure changes (prose only, no EX-21 in either filing)

- Both older filings name **Arrowhead Madison, Inc.** (Delaware) repeatedly via lease/license exhibits (FY2024 lines 3539, 3602-3624; FY2023 lines 3413, 3470) but never enumerate a full subsidiary list the way the FY2025 EX-21 does.
- Neither FY2023 nor FY2024 prose names Arrowhead Australia Pty Ltd, Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals NZ Limited, or Arrowhead Ireland Limited anywhere in the searched text — "New Zealand" and "Australia" hits in both are clinical-trial-geography or patent-jurisdiction references only, not entity names. Cannot confirm from prose whether these three entities existed yet at FY2023/FY2024; baseline (FY2025) is the first point they're confirmed (via EX-21).
- FY2024 explicitly frames Visirna as "a subsidiary of the Company" in the ESOP note (line 6227) and references "material subsidiaries...including foreign subsidiaries" as Sixth Street collateral/guarantors (line 7029) — first appearance of foreign-subsidiary guarantor language, tied to the Aug 7, 2024 Financing Agreement (post-dates FY2023).
- No corporate re-domestication, name change, or Delaware certificate amendment in either filing beyond the standing April 6, 2016 Amended & Restated Certificate of Incorporation (both filings cite the same exhibit, FY2024 line 3421, FY2023 line 3314) plus the same April 27, 2023 certificate-of-amendment (FY2023 line 5519) already reflected as historical in FY2024.

## 4. Officer changes

- **No CEO/CFO/Chairman change between FY2023 and FY2024.** Both signature pages (FY2023 lines 3565-3603; FY2024 lines 3745-3767) show identical officer/director signers: Christopher Anzalone (CEO/President/Director), **Kenneth A. Myszkowski** (CFO, Principal Financial and Accounting Officer — signed both Nov 29, 2023 and Nov 26, 2024), **Douglass Given** (Director, Chairman of the Board — NOT "Douglass Ingram"; signed both years), Mauro Ferrari, Michael S. Perry, William Waddill, Adeoye Olukotun, Victoria Vakiener (Directors, both years).
- **Correction to baseline**: the FY2025 10-K baseline flagged a possible board interlock via a director named "Douglass Ingram" (matching Sarepta's former CEO's name) and asked for proxy confirmation. Both FY2023 and FY2024 signature pages consistently and clearly show the director/Chairman's name as **Douglass Given**, not Ingram — this is very likely an OCR/extraction error in the baseline note, not a real Sarepta board interlock. Recommend correcting the FY2025 baseline entity name to "Douglass Given."
- FY2024 10b5-1 trading-plan table (lines 3333-3367) additionally shows **James Hamilton**, titled "Chief of Discovery and Translational Medicine" (not yet "Chief Medical Officer, Head of R&D" as in FY2025 baseline) — suggests a title change/expansion for Hamilton sometime between FY2024 and FY2025 filings. Officer transition to Daniel Apel (CFO) and Ken Myszkowski's retirement (both noted in baseline as May 2025 events) postdate both older filings; Myszkowski is CFO of record in both.

## 5. Auditor change — CONFIRMED, dated precisely

Both older filings are consistent with baseline: **Rose, Snyder & Jacobs LLP (Encino, California)** audited through FY2023; **KPMG LLP (San Diego, California)** took over starting with the FY2024 audit.

- FY2023 10-K (filed Nov 29, 2023) — sole auditor, Rose, Snyder & Jacobs LLP. Audit-opinion signature block (line 3665-3668):
  > "Rose, Snyder & Jacobs LLP
  > We have served as the Company's auditor since 2004.
  > Encino, California
  > November 29, 2023"

  ICFR-opinion signature block (line 3686-3688): "Rose, Snyder & Jacobs LLP / Encino, CA / November 29, 2023."

- FY2024 10-K (filed Nov 26, 2024) — **contains both auditors' reports** (KPMG for FY2024; Rose, Snyder & Jacobs's FY2023 report reprinted for the prior-year comparative). KPMG's opinion signature block (lines 3835-3838):
  > "KPMG LLP
  > We have served as the Company's auditor since 2024.
  > San Diego, CA
  > November 26, 2024"

  KPMG's ICFR-opinion block (3852-3854): "KPMG LLP / San Diego, CA / November 26, 2024." Immediately following (lines 3856-3867), the FY2024 filing reprints Rose, Snyder & Jacobs LLP's prior-year opinion on the Sept 30, 2023 balance sheet, again signed "Encino, California / November 29, 2023" and stating "We have served as the Company's auditor from 2004 to 2023" (line 3864-3865) — i.e. Rose Snyder's own report already frames 2023 as its last year even before the FY2024 filing existed.
- **Precision available from the filings**: the switch is dated to the fiscal-year boundary, not a specific mid-year engagement date — KPMG states its tenure began "since 2024" (first audit = FY2024, fiscal year ended Sept 30, 2024, report dated Nov 26, 2024); Rose, Snyder & Jacobs states its tenure ran "from 2004 to 2023" (last audit = FY2023, fiscal year ended Sept 30, 2023, report dated Nov 29, 2023). No 8-K Item 4.01 auditor-change disclosure is in this text corpus to pin down an exact dismissal/engagement date more precisely than "between the Nov 29, 2023 and Nov 26, 2024 filings, effective for the FY2024 audit."
