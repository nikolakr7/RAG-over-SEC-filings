## 8-K EVENTS (chronological)

1. **2026-02-23 — Item 8.01 — FDA accepts DTX401 BLA with Priority Review.** FDA accepted the BLA for DTX401 (pariglasgene brecaparvovec), AAV8 gene therapy for Glycogen Storage Disease Type Ia (GSDIa); Priority Review granted; **PDUFA date August 23, 2026**. Parties: Ultragenyx, FDA.

2. **2026-03-12 — Item 8.01 — Positive Phase 3 Enh3ance data for DTX301 (OTC deficiency).** Statistically significant 18% (p=0.018) reduction in 24-hour plasma ammonia vs placebo at Week 36 (n=18 treated vs n=19 placebo); 8 of 9 patients with abnormal baseline ammonia normalized; one treatment-related SAE (acute hepatitis, resolved with steroids); one placebo-arm death from hyperammonemic crisis. Study continues to second primary endpoint (treatment-burden reduction) with data expected 1H 2027, managed within the 2027-profitability goal. Parties: Ultragenyx (DTX301 program ex-Dimension Therapeutics).

3. **2026-03-30 — Item 8.01 — IND cleared for UX016 (GNE myopathy), new program.** FDA cleared the IND for UX016, a small-molecule prodrug of sialic acid (substrate replacement) for GNE myopathy (GNEM). Notably, the program is **externally funded by a patient group through clinical proof-of-concept**; Phase 1/2 (~24 adults, US) to begin 2H 2026. Parties: Ultragenyx, FDA, unnamed patient group (philanthropic funder).

4. **2026-04-02 — Item 8.01 — FDA accepts resubmitted UX111 BLA.** FDA accepted for review the resubmitted BLA seeking accelerated approval of UX111 (rebisufligene etisparvovec), AAV9 gene therapy for Sanfilippo syndrome Type A (MPS IIIA); **PDUFA date September 19, 2026**. (Follows the July 2025 CRL and Feb 2026 IRL in the 10-K baseline.) Parties: Ultragenyx, FDA; program licensed from Abeona Therapeutics.

5. **2026-05-05 — Item 2.02 — Q1 2026 earnings press release** (results for three months ended March 31, 2026). Earnings only.

6. **2026-05-18 (event 2026-05-14) — Items 5.02/5.07 — Annual Meeting results.** Class I directors re-elected to terms expiring 2029: Emil D. Kakkis, Shehnaaz Suliman, Daniel G. Welch. Third Amended and Restated 2023 Incentive Plan approved (notably close: 50.7M for / 22.9M against; adds 3.0M shares, term to May 14, 2036). Ernst & Young LLP ratified as auditor for FY2026. Say-on-pay approved.

7. **2026-08-04 — Item 2.02 — Q2 2026 earnings press release** (results for three months ended June 30, 2026). Earnings only.

8. **2026-08-11 (event 2026-08-07) — Item 8.01 — Dojolvi ANDA litigation settlement with Esjay.** Ultragenyx and co-plaintiff **Baylor Research Institute (BRI)** settled Hatch-Waxman patent litigation (D.N.J.) with **Esjay Pharma Private Limited / Esjay Pharma LLC**: Esjay receives a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to market generic DOJOLVI (triheptanoin) beginning **January 1, 2033** (July 1, 2033 if pediatric exclusivity is granted). Settlement to be submitted to FTC and DOJ. Similar litigation against other ANDA filers (Navinta, Aurobindo per 10-K) remains pending.
