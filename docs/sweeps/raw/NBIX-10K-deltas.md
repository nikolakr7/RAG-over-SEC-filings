## DEAL DELTAS

### Idorsia Pharmaceuticals Ltd. — agreement present FY2023-FY2024, ABSENT from FY2025 baseline (terminated)
- FY2023 (~L3521-3530): 2020 collaboration and license agreement; NBIX acquired **global rights to NBI-827104**, a T-type calcium channel blocker for a rare pediatric epilepsy and other indications incl. essential tremor. Idorsia eligible for potential future payments **up to $1.7B** + royalties. NBIX may terminate on 90 days' notice.
- FY2024 (~L3703-3705, highlight ~L2115): in Q4 2024 NBIX gave Idorsia written **notice of termination**; termination **effective January 2025**.
- FY2025 baseline: Idorsia not mentioned at all. Graph edge: NBIX–Idorsia license 2020 -> terminated Jan 2025.

### Takeda — three-state evolution of the 2020 Takeda Agreement
- FY2023 (~L3499-3511): compounds listed as "luvadaxistat, NBI-1065845, NBI-1065846 and **four** non-clinical stage compounds". Luvadaxistat + the four non-clinical compounds = royalty-bearing; **NBI-1065845 AND NBI-1065846 both designated profit-share products** (50/50 operating profits/losses), with Takeda holding opt-out rights. Potential future payments up to $1.9B. Milestone history: $5.0M paid 2022 (NBI-1070770 CTA approval).
- FY2024 (~L3677-3702; highlights ~L2080-2082, L2113): luvadaxistat ERUDITE Phase 2 (CIAS) missed primary endpoint in 2024; NBIX gave Takeda **termination notice in 2024 for luvadaxistat and NBI-1065846 (DAAO inhibitors / GPR139 agonist), effective April 2025**. **Restated Takeda Agreement signed January 2025**: osavampator converted from profit-share to royalty-bearing license; **Japan rights to osavampator returned to Takeda**; NBIX keeps exclusive worldwide ex-Japan; tiered royalties to Takeda mid-to-upper teens (U.S.) / low double digits (ex-U.S.); NBIX to receive royalties on Takeda Japan sales. Potential payments still stated at **$1.9B "excluding the effects of the Restated Takeda Agreement and any pending terminations"** (~L3690-3692). Milestone paid 2024: $7.5M (NBI-1070770 Phase 2 start). Upcoming: Phase 3 initiation of osavampator stated to trigger a **$35.0M** milestone (~L3692-3694) — baseline FY2025 reports **$37.5M actually paid in 2025**; flag the $35.0M vs $37.5M figure change.
- FY2025 baseline: potential remaining milestones to Takeda down to $0.74B. Naming: FY2023 uses only "NBI-1065845"; **"osavampator (formerly NBI-1065845)" first appears in FY2024** (~L2080, L3679).

### Nxera / Heptares — counterparty renamed and milestones accreting
- FY2023 (~L3477-3498): counterparty is **"Heptares Therapeutics Limited, or Heptares"** (no Nxera/Sosei name used). Agreement effective **December 2021**; **$100.0M upfront paid, expensed as IPR&D in 2021** (~L3479-3481, also L2171). Milestone: $30.0M (2022, IND acceptance NBI-1117568). Potential future payments **up to $2.6B**. Terms at signing: NBIX exclusive worldwide ex-Japan (Heptares retains Japan M1 rights); NBIX 50/50 profit-share opt-in for Japan; termination on 180 days' (research term) / 90 days' notice.
- FY2024 (~L3652-3675, highlight ~L2092): counterparty now **"Nxera Pharma UK Limited, or Nxera (formerly Sosei Heptares)"**. Milestones paid 2024: **$50.0M total** (long-term tox program + Phase 2 completion for NBI-1117568; the Phase 2 completion piece was $35.0M per ~L2092). Potential future payments now **up to $2.5B**. Expected 1H 2025 Phase 3 start to trigger $15.0M (paid in 2025 per baseline).
- Naming: NBI-1117568 is never called **direclidine** in FY2023 or FY2024; the INN first appears in the FY2025 baseline.

### Voyager — 2019 Agreement potential milestones collapsed after program returns
- FY2023 (~L3573-3594) and FY2024 (~L3728-3752): 2019 Voyager Agreement potential future payments **up to $1.3B** (FA program + two undisclosed programs intact). FY2024 adds: $5.0M milestone expensed 2024 for FA development candidate selection (~L3742-3744).
- FY2025 baseline: potential payments only **$0.47B** after the **two undisclosed programs were discontinued and targets returned to Voyager in April 2025**. Dated edge: scope reduction between Feb 2025 and Feb 2026 filings.
- 2023 Voyager Agreement consistent across years ($175M upfront incl. 4.4M shares @$8.88; $143.9M IPR&D in 2023; up to $6.1B milestones). FY2023 (~L3612-3616): Onyia's initial Voyager board term "expiring in 2024"; ~19.9% ownership, related party from 2023. FY2024 notes April 2024 amendment (~L3754) and $6.0M milestones for two GBA1 development candidate selections in 2024 (~L3779-3783). FY2023 gives investment fair value $72.4M at 2023-12-31 (~L3626-3628).

### Sanofi — deal economics surface only upon approval
- FY2023: **no Sanofi section in the collaboration note (Note 2)**; Sanofi appears only as pipeline footnote "(4) This program was in-licensed from Sanofi S.A." (~L1167). Crinecerfont then unapproved: positive Phase 3 topline + Breakthrough Therapy designation in 2023, NDA planned Q2 2024 (~L1995).
- FY2024 (~L3792-3805): full Sanofi note first appears after **FDA approval of CRENESSITY (Dec 2024)**: $5.0M milestone accrued (paid Jan 2025), remaining potential milestones up to $10.0M, **royalties 3.0-5.0% tiered on U.S. net sales for the longer of 16 years or patent life** — same economics as baseline.

### MTPC / Tanabe — counterparty renamed; deal terms fuller in older filings
- FY2023 (~L3644-3660) and FY2024 (~L3806-3824): counterparty is **"Mitsubishi Tanabe Pharma Corporation (MTPC)"**; FY2025 baseline uses **"Tanabe Pharma Corporation (TPC), formerly Mitsubishi Tanabe"** — rename occurred between the FY2024 and FY2025 filings.
- Terms preserved in older filings (compressed in baseline): 2015 out-license (Japan + select Asian markets); **2020 commercial supply agreement** (NBIX supplies valbenazine drug product); **$20.0M milestone received 2022** on DYSVAL first commercial sale in Japan; potential future sales-based milestones **up to $30.0M**; royalty term the longer of **10 years or patent life**; MTPC may terminate on 180 days' notice with rights reverting to NBIX. DYSVAL **launched in Japan June 2022** (baseline instead cites March 2022 approval).

### AbbVie — quantified terms in older filings
- FY2024 (~L3825-3845; same in FY2023 ~L3660s): 2010 out-license of global elagolix rights. Potential future event-based milestones to NBIX **up to $366.0M**; royalty term the longer of **10 years or patent life**; AbbVie may terminate on 180 days' notice with rights reverting. Elagolix royalty revenue: **$21.2M (2022), $16.7M (2023), $13.5M (2024)** (~L3832-3838 FY2024). Baseline omits the $366M and royalty-term specifics.

### BIAL — termination detail

## LITIGATION DELTAS

### INGREZZA ANDA litigation — full detail in FY2023 (Note 13, ~L5562-5566), compressed in later years
- Paragraph IV notices received **during 2021, 2022, and 2023** from: (i) Teva Pharmaceuticals Development, Inc., (ii) Lupin Limited, (iii) Crystal Pharmaceutical (Suzhou) Co. Ltd., (iv) Sandoz Inc., (v) Zydus Pharmaceuticals (USA) Inc.
- NBIX sued in **D. Del. during 2021-2023**: Teva group (Teva Pharmaceuticals, Inc.; Teva Pharmaceuticals Development, Inc.; Teva Pharmaceuticals USA, Inc.; Teva Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd. — dismissed); Lupin group (Lupin Limited; Lupin Pharmaceuticals, Inc.; Lupin Inc.; Lupin Atlantis Holdings S.A.); Crystal group (Crystal Pharmaceutical (Suzhou) Co., Ltd.; Crystal Pharmatech Co., Ltd.); Sandoz group (Sandoz Inc.; Sandoz International GmbH — dismissed; Sandoz AG — dismissed); Zydus group (Zydus Pharmaceuticals (USA) Inc.; Zydus Worldwide DMCC; **Zydus Lifesciences Limited f/k/a Cadila Healthcare Limited d/b/a Zydus Cadila**; Zydus Healthcare (USA) LLC — dismissed). Parallel **D.N.J. suits vs Zydus 2021-2023**.
- **2023 settlements** with (i) Sandoz + Crystal ("Sandoz Parties"), (ii) Teva, (iii) Lupin, (iv) Zydus: each may sell generic INGREZZA in the U.S. **beginning March 1, 2038** or earlier in certain circumstances; all cases dismissed. FY2024 keeps only a one-paragraph IP-section summary (~L1268); baseline the same.

### Spruce Biosciences — present ONLY in FY2024 (~L5707-5708), absent from FY2025 baseline
- **January 2025**: NBIX filed suit in **D. Del. against Spruce Biosciences, Inc.** seeking to invalidate a Spruce patent; also initiated (1) administrative proceedings against other Spruce patents at the USPTO and (2) judicial and administrative proceedings against Spruce patents in other jurisdictions. (Spruce developed tildacerfont, a competing CRF-1 antagonist for CAH.) Disappearance by the FY2025 filing implies resolution/withdrawal during 2025 — worth an external check for the terminal event.

### Other
