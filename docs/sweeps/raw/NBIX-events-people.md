## 8-K EVENTS (chronological)

1. **2025-10-28 — Q3 2025 earnings (Item 2.02).** Announced financial results for Q3 ended Sept 30, 2025. Signed by CFO Matthew C. Abernethy.
2. **2025-11-24 (event 2025-11-21) — Officer employment amendment (Item 5.02(e)).** Amendment to the Amended and Restated Employment Agreement of **Eiry W. Roberts, M.D.** Context restated: Sanjay Keswani, M.D. succeeded Roberts as Chief Medical Officer effective **June 2, 2025**; Roberts became **Strategic Advisor** through Dec 31, 2025 under an A&R employment agreement effective June 2, 2025. The Nov 21, 2025 amendment **extends her Strategic Advisor term 12 months, to Dec 31, 2026**. Signed by Chief Legal Officer Darin M. Lippoldt.
3. **2026-02-11 — Q4/FY2025 earnings (Item 2.02).** Announced results for Q4 and fiscal year ended Dec 31, 2025. Signed by CFO Abernethy.
4. **2026-04-06 (event 2026-04-05) — Merger agreement to acquire Soleno Therapeutics (Items 1.01, 7.01).** Neurocrine entered an Agreement and Plan of Merger dated **April 5, 2026** with **Soleno Therapeutics, Inc.** (Delaware) and Sigma Merger Sub, Inc. (wholly owned NBIX subsidiary): all-cash tender offer at **$53.00 per Soleno share**, followed by back-end merger under DGCL 251(h). No financing condition. Termination fee $95.25M (Soleno to NBIX); reverse termination fee $141.5M (NBIX to Soleno, antitrust). Tender and Support Agreements signed by **Anish Bhatnagar** (Soleno CEO and Chairman) and **James Mackaness** (Soleno former CFO), together ~1.01% of shares. Soleno's commercial product: **VYKAT XR (diazoxide choline)** (Prader-Willi syndrome context; drug named in forward-looking statements). Joint press release + investor presentation Apr 6, 2026. Signed by Lippoldt.
5. **2026-05-05 — Q1 2026 earnings (Item 2.02).** Announced results for Q1 ended March 31, 2026. Signed by CFO Abernethy.
6. **2026-05-18 (events 2026-05-14 to 2026-05-18) — Soleno acquisition completed + $1.0B credit facility (Items 1.01, 2.01, 2.03, 7.01).**
   - Tender offer expired May 15, 2026: 46,356,114 Soleno shares tendered (~**88.9%** of outstanding); depositary Equiniti Trust Company, LLC. Merger completed **May 18, 2026**; Soleno is now a **direct wholly owned subsidiary of Neurocrine**. Aggregate cash paid: **~$2.9 billion**, funded from cash on hand. Soleno options/RSUs cashed out; warrants treated as cashless-exercised.
   - **Credit Agreement dated May 14, 2026 with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A.** (administrative and collateral agent) and lenders: five-year **$1.0B senior secured revolving credit facility** (Term SOFR + 1.125-1.75%); security interest in substantially all assets; covenants: max total net leverage 3.75:1.00 (4.25 post-acquisition step-up), min interest coverage 2.00:1.00. **Initial borrowing $600.0M** on closing date. Signed by Lippoldt.
7. **2026-05-28 (event 2026-05-27) — 2026 Annual Meeting results (Items 5.02(e), 5.07).** Stockholders: (i) elected Class III directors **Kevin C. Gorman, Ph.D., Gary A. Lyons, Johanna Mercier** (terms to 2029); (ii) approved say-on-pay; (iii) approved the **Amended 2025 Equity Incentive Plan**; (iv) ratified **Ernst & Young LLP** as auditor for FY2026. Continuing: Class I (Rastetter, Morrow, Norwalk, Poon — to 2027), Class II (Gano, Pops, Sharp, Sherwin — to 2028). Signed by Lippoldt.
8. **2026-07-30 — Q2 2026 earnings (Item 2.02).** Announced results for Q2 ended June 30, 2026. Signed by CFO Abernethy.
