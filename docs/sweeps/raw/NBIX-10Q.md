### Soleno Therapeutics acquisition (COMPLETED) — new entity, new product, new subsidiary
- **May 18, 2026: NBIX completed acquisition of Soleno Therapeutics, Inc. (Soleno)** via cash tender offer followed by merger, **$53.00 per share cash**; Soleno is now a wholly owned subsidiary (L2057-2060). All-cash transaction, total equity value ~$2.9B (L3418).
- Preliminary consideration transferred: **$2,829.9M** ($2,762.5M to stockholders + $67.4M to settle Soleno equity awards for pre-combination service) (L2072-2085). Cash paid net of cash acquired: $2.36B (L3934). Funded partly by liquidating debt-security investments (L3899).
- Soleno = commercial-stage rare disease company marketing **VYKAT XR (diazoxide choline)**, "the first and only FDA-approved treatment for hyperphagia in Prader-Willi syndrome (PWS)" (L2061-2063, L3408-3410). NBIX now has three commercial products: INGREZZA, CRENESSITY, VYKAT XR (L4054).
- Purchase price allocation: intangible assets (VYKAT XR developed product rights) **$2,237.8M**, amortized straight-line over **16 years**, valued via probability-adjusted DCF at 24.0% discount rate; goodwill **$494.6M** (not tax deductible); inventory fair-value step-ups $41.5M WIP + $126.7M finished goods, to be expensed over ~4-6 years; deferred tax liabilities $(489.4)M (L2087-2154, L3837).
- Transaction costs $33.1M (financial advisory + legal, in SG&A) (L2156-2158); $60.1M stock-comp expense from accelerated Soleno equity awards (post-combination service) (L2067-2069); $96.8M total acquisition-related expenses incl. $40.2M stock comp (L3803).
- Soleno results from acquisition date to 2026-06-30: net revenues $54.4M, net loss $52.2M (incl. $104.7M acquisition-related items) (L2162-2169).
- New risk factor: "We may not successfully integrate Soleno... or realize the anticipated benefits" (L4062, L4185-4187).

### New financing — JPMorgan credit facility
- **May 14, 2026: credit agreement with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as administrative agent** for a **$1.0B senior secured revolving credit facility (the 2026 Credit Facility), maturing May 14, 2031** (L2486-2488). Borrowed $600.0M May 14, 2026; repaid in full June 2026 ($2.7M accrued interest); zero outstanding at 2026-06-30 (L2498-2508). Initial all-in Term SOFR rate 4.90% (3.65% + 1.25% margin); 0.15% commitment fee (L2489-2497). Secured by liens on substantially all assets of NBIX and subsidiary guarantors; leverage and interest-coverage covenants (L2519).
