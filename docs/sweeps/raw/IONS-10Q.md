## Q2 2026 (period ended 2026-06-30; filed 2026-07-29)

Source: `10-Q_2026-07-29_000087401526000251.txt`

### New/amended agreements [POST-10-K]
- **NEW PARTNER: Recordati AG** — June 2026 agreement to commercialize **zilganersen** (Alexander disease) in countries outside the U.S.; Ionis retains U.S. rights and global development. Up to $75M total: $30M upfront (received July 2026), up to $15M regulatory + $30M sales milestones; tiered royalties up to mid-20% range on net sales; clinical cost reimbursements ex-U.S. after March 2028. Two performance obligations: $21.0M license (recognized Q2 2026) + $9.0M R&D services (recognizing through ~Q2 2031). Next payment up to $10M on first ex-U.S. approval. (~2650-2690)
- **GSK CHB agreement AMENDED Q2 2026** (Amendment #9 dated **April 2, 2026** to the March 30, 2010 Research, Development and License Agreement, exhibit ~6627): increased milestones, and royalty terms amended to **exclude net sales in China, Hong Kong and Macau**. Now eligible for >$510M total ($25M license fee, up to $46.5M development, $320M regulatory, $120M sales milestones); tiered royalties 10-12% on bepirovirsen worldwide net sales ex-China/HK/Macau. >$135M received to date. Next: $35M if bepirovirsen approved in a major country other than China. (~2409-2435)
- **AstraZeneca WAINUA collaboration: Ionis EXERCISED ITS OPT-OUT RIGHT**; "the opt-out process set forth in the agreement is underway" (disclosed only in risk factors; no date or terms given). Note the note-4 accounting language also shifted to past tense ("shared ... activities that we and AstraZeneca **were to perform**"). (~6136, ~2276-2300)

### Litigation [POST-10-K]
- **NEW SUIT: SPINRAZA generic (Somerset)** — On **June 1, 2026**, Biogen received notice that **Somerset Therapeutics, LLC** filed an ANDA seeking approval of a generic SPINRAZA intrathecal injection 12 mg/5 ml, alleging patents covering SPINRAZA are invalid/not infringed. On **June 22, 2026**, **Biogen, Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory and Ionis jointly sued** Somerset for patent infringement in the District of Delaware; ongoing. (~4271-4273, risk factor ~6330)
- **Ionis v. Arrowhead (plozasiran, patent US 9,593,333)** — state unchanged from Q1 2026: Arrowhead is commercializing plozasiran, suit ongoing in C.D. Cal.; Arrowhead has filed its Answer and Counterclaims (invalidity/non-infringement). (~4271, ~6338)

### Dated events [POST-10-K]
- **June 2026: FDA approved TRYNGOLZA (olezarsen) for sHTG** (PDUFA was June 30, 2026); launched immediately. In **April 2026 Ionis lowered TRYNGOLZA's Wholesale Acquisition Cost (WAC)** ahead of approval to align with 2027 payer contracting; consequence visible in revenue: TRYNGOLZA net sales fell to **$4.6M in Q2 2026** (vs $19.3M Q2 2025; $31.7M 1H 2026). DAWNZERA net sales $26.6M Q2 2026. (~2011-2013, ~2230-2233, ~2084-2100)
- **July 2026: CARDIO-TTRansform Phase 3 MISSED its primary endpoint** — eplontersen in ATTR-CM (with AstraZeneca) did not show statistically significant benefit on composite CV mortality + recurrent CV events at Week 140; full data at ESC Congress August 2026. (Kills the $200M/$115M ATTR-CM approval milestones from baseline; pairs with the WAINUA opt-out above.) (~4502-4503, ~6190)
- **Q2 2026 milestones earned**: **$45M from Biogen** (initiated Phase 3 of salanersen for SMA; next $55M on major-market approval; $130M received under 2017 SMA collab to date) (~2314-2330); **$15M license fee from Biogen** exercising its option to license **an investigational ALS candidate** under the 2018 neurology collaboration (next $7.5M on advancement) (~2336-2350); **$20M from Ono** (initiated pivotal trial of sapablursen for PV; next $15M on FDA NDA acceptance; >$305M received from Ono) (~2500-2520).
- **Biogen = 50% of Q2 2026 total revenue** ($133.9M of $267.9M; 38% for 1H 2026). GSK $30M = 6% 1H. Total revenue Q2 2026 $267.9M vs $452.0M Q2 2025 (Ono $280M upfront in prior-year quarter). (~2370-2400, ~4618)
- **April 2026: 0% Notes due 2026 repaid at maturity** (funded via escrow); related convertible note hedges exercised April 2026 (shares received and retired); warrants (10.9M shares) exercisable from July 1, 2026, expiring through September 2026. (~5615, ~5819, ~4232-4245, ~6478)
- Pipeline dates: obudanersen Phase 3 REVEAL **enrollment completed July 2026** (~4491); bepirovirsen **FDA Priority Review, PDUFA October 26, 2026**, positive Phase 3 data at EASL May 2026 (~4499); zilganersen **PDUFA September 22, 2026** (~4484); higher-dose SPINRAZA now "approved and launched in the U.S. and EU" (~4471).
