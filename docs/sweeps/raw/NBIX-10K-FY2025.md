### Drugs / compounds (brand vs generic)
- **INGREZZA (valbenazine)** — flagship; selective VMAT2 inhibitor. FDA-approved: tardive dyskinesia (TD) May 2017 (first FDA-approved TD drug); chorea associated with Huntington's disease Aug 2023 (~L1085). Also **INGREZZA SPRINKLE** formulation (subject of Zydus ANDA, ~L5797). Net product sales: $2.51B (2025), $2.31B (2024), $1.84B (2023) (~L1088). 22 Orange Book patents expiring 2027-2040; composition patent 8,039,627 extended to 2031 (~L1167).
- **DYSVAL (valbenazine)** — brand in Japan; **REMLEAS (valbenazine)** — brand in other select Asian markets; both commercialized by Tanabe Pharma Corporation (~L1106-1110). DYSVAL approved in Japan for TD March 2022 (~L1596).
- **CRENESSITY (crinecerfont)** — oral CRF-1 receptor antagonist; first-in-class FDA-approved treatment of classic congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH), approved/launched Dec 2024; $301.2M net sales in 2025 (first full launch year) (~L1087-1088, L2093). In-licensed from Sanofi (2014). 4 Orange Book patents 2035-2041; 7-year orphan exclusivity (~L1169). Royalties 3-5% tiered payable to Sanofi (~L5586-5594).
- **elagolix** — GnRH antagonist; AbbVie retains global commercialization rights (~L1111). Brands: **ORILISSA** (endometriosis, FDA approved July 2018) and **ORIAHNN** (uterine fibroids, FDA approved May 2020) (~L1514, L1596). NBIX receives royalties (collaboration revenues, ~L2120).
- **ONGENTYS (opicapone)** — former product; license with BIAL terminated effective December 2023 (~L2144-2146).
- **ALKINDI** and **EFMODY** — products of the Diurnal (Neurocrine Group Limited) unit; net sales in "other revenues" (~L5791); divested with the Immedica sale.
- Pipeline compounds:

## NOTABLE

### Five most concrete named risk factors
1. **Teva competition / IRA price negotiation spillover**: INGREZZA competes with Teva's AUSTEDO/AUSTEDO XR; AUSTEDO selected for Medicare price negotiation in 2025 (MFP effective 2027), which may pressure INGREZZA pricing and formulary position (~L1248, L1392).
2. **DOJ civil investigative demand (Aug 2025)** over INGREZZA sales and marketing practices (~L1203, L5801).
3. **FDA untitled letter (Sept 2025)** from Office of Prescription Drug Promotion alleging misleading INGREZZA promotional claims, amid MAHA-driven DTC advertising crackdown (~L1618).
4. **Revenue concentration**: four customers represent over 90% of total gross product sales; CRENESSITY distributed through a single specialty pharmacy (~L1562-1563).
5. **Loss of IRA small-manufacturer exemptions**: INGREZZA qualifies for the "small biotech exception" (exempt from negotiation selection until 2027/price year 2029) and NBIX is a "specified small manufacturer" for Part D discount phase-in; both could be lost, including if a third party acquires NBIX (~L1249-1251).
