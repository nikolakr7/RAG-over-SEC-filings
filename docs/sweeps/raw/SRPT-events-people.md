## 8-K EVENTS (chronological)

1. **2025-12-19 (event 2025-12-18) — December 2025 convertible note exchange closes** (`8-K_2025-12-19_000119312525326969.txt`, Items 1.01, 2.03). Under Exchange Agreements dated Dec 10, 2025, holders of the 1.25% Convertible Senior Notes due 2027 — "including the Michael A. Chambers Living Trust, an entity affiliated with Michael Chambers, a member of the Company's board of directors" — exchanged ~$291.4M principal of 2027 Notes for ~$291.4M of new 4.875% Convertible Senior Notes due 2030 plus ~$31.6M cash. New notes issued Dec 18, 2025 under the Aug 28, 2025 Indenture (First Supplemental Indenture, Dec 18, 2025) with U.S. Bank Trust Company, National Association as trustee. Total 2030 Notes outstanding after close: ~$893.4M. Signed by Ian Estepan, President and COO. (Matches the "December 2025 Exchange" in the 10-K sweep; related-party angle confirmed in the 2026 proxy: the Chambers Trust's slice was $20.0M of 2027 Notes for $20.0M of 2030 Notes + ~$2.2M cash.)

2. **2026-01-12 — Business update / preliminary FY2025 results** (Item 2.02) issued in connection with the 44th Annual J.P. Morgan Healthcare Conference. Earnings-only. Signed by CEO Douglas S. Ingram.

3. **2026-02-25 — Q4 and FY2025 financial results** (Item 2.02, accession ...071566). Earnings-only. Signed by Ingram.

4. **2026-02-25 — CEO retirement notice** (Item 5.02, accession ...072064): "On February 25, 2026, Douglas Ingram notified Sarepta Therapeutics, Inc. of his decision to retire as Chief Executive Officer by the end of 2026 or upon the appointment of his replacement." Internal and external candidate search commenced. Signed by Cristin L. Rothfuss, EVP, General Counsel.

5. **2026-05-06 — Q1 2026 financial results** (Item 2.02). Earnings-only. Signed by Ingram.

6. **2026-06-04 — Annual meeting results** (Items 5.02, 5.07). Stockholders approved the 2026 Equity Incentive Plan (6,286,841 shares; supersedes the 2018 Plan) and the 2026 ESPP (1,500,000 shares; supersedes the 2013 ESPP). Elected as Class I directors to serve until the 2028 annual meeting: Douglas S. Ingram, Hans Wigzell, Kathryn J. Boor, Michael Chambers, Deirdre Connelly. Say-on-pay passed narrowly (45.2M for / 22.4M against). KPMG LLP ratified as auditor for FY2026. Record date Apr 8, 2026: 105,571,146 shares outstanding.

7. **2026-07-27 (events 2026-07-23/28) — CEO succession: Michael Severino, M.D. appointed CEO** (Items 5.02, 7.01). On July 23, 2026 the Board appointed Michael Severino, M.D. (age 60) as CEO and Class I director (term to 2028 annual meeting), effective July 28, 2026, succeeding Douglas Ingram, who departed as CEO and Board member the same date. Ingram signed a Consulting Agreement (dated July 26, 2026): consultant through Dec 31, 2026 at $15,000/month. Severino background (verbatim from 8-K): most recently CEO and board member of Tessera Therapeutics, Inc. and "CEO-Partner at Flagship Pioneering from June 2022 to July 2026"; before that "Vice Chairman and President of AbbVie Inc. ... from December 2018 until June 2022, where he was responsible for research and development and oversaw corporate strategy. At AbbVie, he also served as Executive Vice President of Research and Development, Chief Scientific Officer. Prior to joining AbbVie, Dr. Severino served in roles of increasing responsibility at Amgen, Inc., leading to his appointment as Senior Vice President, Global Development and Chief Medical Officer." Current outside boards: Viatris Inc. (chair of science & technology committee, member of audit committee) and Avantor, Inc. (chair of compensation and human resources committee). Employment terms: 3-year initial term, base $1,180,000, target bonus 110%, ~$35M sign-on equity grant (incl. $14M make-whole for forfeited prior-employer equity), 1/3 RSUs and 2/3 premium-priced options.

8. **2026-08-05 — Q2 2026 financial results** (Item 2.02). Earnings-only. First 8-K signed by Michael Severino, M.D., Chief Executive Officer.

## CROSS-COMPANY PEOPLE LINKS

### Ingram–Arrowhead (ARWR, in corpus) — THE SPECIFIC CHECK
- 2025 DEF 14A (filed 2025-04-24), Ingram bio, line 1010: "Mr. Ingram also currently serves on the board of Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, Inc." — stated as a bare fact; the 2025 proxy gives NO rationale and does NOT tie the seat to the November 2024 collaboration anywhere (the proxy's Arrowhead discussion covers only the license deal itself as a 2024 corporate achievement).
- 2026 DEF 14A (filed 2026-04-24), Ingram bio, line 991: "Mr. Ingram previously served on the board of Arrowhead Pharmaceuticals, Inc. from February 2025 to March 2026." — again no rationale, and it dates the seat's END as March 2026, slightly earlier than the 10-K's "term ends at Arrowhead's 2026 annual meeting" framing.
- The rationale linking the seat to the deal appears only in the FY2025 10-K (line 10096 per baseline sweep): appointed to Arrowhead's board effective Feb 2025 "as part of the collaboration"; not standing for re-election. Neither proxy treats the seat as a related-party transaction or interlock (2026 proxy explicitly states no compensation committee interlocks).
- Timeline note: Ingram left Arrowhead's board (March 2026) within weeks of announcing his Sarepta retirement (Feb 25, 2026).
