### Approved product and drug candidates (brand vs generic vs legacy codes)
- **REDEMPLO® (plozasiran)** — brand contains compound plozasiran, formerly **ARO-APOC3**. siRNA against APOC3. FDA-approved November 18, 2025 (after FY-end) for Familial Chylomicronemia Syndrome (FCS), based on Phase 3 PALISADE trial (lines 1564-1604, 2986, 8021). First Arrowhead commercial product. sNDA for severe hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG) planned 2026 (SHASTA-3/4/5, MUIR-3 Phase 3s, lines 1566, 1623). - **fazirsiran** (formerly **TAK-999** and ARO-AAT) — AAT/Z-AAT; co-developed with Takeda; Phase 3 REDWOOD (lines 1667-1673).
- **GSK-4532990 / GSK4532990** (formerly ARO-HSD) — HSD17B13, MASH/ALD; licensed to GSK, Phase 2b (lines 1656-1663).

- **ARO-PNPLA3** (formerly **JNJ-75220795**, also "ARO-JNJ1") — MASH; rights regained from Janssen (lines 1635, 2966, 5726).

### Platform technologies
- **TRiM™ (Targeted RNAi Molecule) platform** — ligand-mediated, tissue-specific siRNA delivery; delivery to seven cell types; components: targeting ligand, linker chemistries, PK enhancers, stabilized RNAi triggers (lines 1588-1596).
- Proprietary RNA chemistries / trigger structures (lines 1597-1599). Delivery platform patent families: CNS Intrathecal, Adipose, BBB Shuttle, Muscle delivery, PK/PD lipid modifiers, αvβ6 integrin ligands, N-acetylgalactosamine (GalNAc) ligands, trialkyne linkers (lines 1748-1766).
- ~643 issued patents, ~833 pending applications, 103 patent families (line 1700).

1. **Ionis '333 patent litigation** could impact planned commercialization of REDEMPLO; possible damages, forced license, or abandonment (lines 2404-2410, summary 2155).
