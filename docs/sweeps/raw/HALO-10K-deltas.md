# HALO 10-K deltas vs FY2025 baseline (docs/sweep-halo.md)

Comparing FY2023 10-K (filed 2024-02-20) and FY2024 10-K (filed 2025-02-18) against
the FY2025 baseline (filed 2026-02-17). Financial tables skipped per instructions.

## 1. ENHANZE partner roster growth (13 partners, first-appearance tracking)

Both older 10-Ks (FY2023 and FY2024) already contain: Roche/Genentech, Takeda, Pfizer,
Janssen, AbbVie, Lilly, BMS, argenx, ViiV, Chugai, Acumen (7 occurrences in both years —
Acumen deal is Nov 2023, so present from the FY2023 filing onward).

**Absent in both FY2023 and FY2024 filings** (confirmed 0 hits): Merus, Skye, Elektrofi,
Surf Bio. This matches baseline: Merus deal Nov 2025, Skye deal Dec 2025, Elektrofi/Surf
Bio acquisitions closed Nov/Dec 2025 — all postdate even the FY2024 (Feb 2025) filing.

So of the 13 ENHANZE partners, **11 were already in place by the FY2023 10-K** (Roche,
Takeda, Pfizer, Janssen, AbbVie, Lilly, BMS, argenx, ViiV, Chugai, Acumen); Merus and
Skye are the only two that first appear in the FY2025 10-K (baseline).

## 2. TLANDO / Lipocine termination

- **FY2023 10-K (2024-02-20)**: termination is FRESH — "notice of termination of the
  TLANDO license agreement provided to Lipocine in September 2023, effective January 31,
  2024," tied to a $2.5M impairment of the TLANDO product-rights intangible (full
  impairment recognized in FY2023). Matches task expectation.
- **FY2024 10-K (2025-02-18)**: TLANDO/Lipocine still referenced only in the historical
  impairment/amortization discussion (prior-year comparison), no new termination language.

## 3. Exhibit 21 subsidiary list, 3-year comparison

- **EX-21 FY2023** (ex211subsidiaries2023.htm) — 4 subsidiaries: Halozyme, Inc. (CA);
  Antares Pharma, Inc. (DE); Antares Pharma IPL AG (Switzerland); **Antares Pharma AG**
  (Switzerland).
- **EX-21 FY2024** (ex211subsidiaries2024.htm) — identical to FY2023: same 4 entities,
  same "Antares Pharma AG" naming.
- **EX-21 FY2025** (baseline, ex211subsidiaries2025.htm) — 7 subsidiaries: Halozyme, Inc.;
  Antares Pharma, Inc.; Antares Pharma IPL AG; **Antares Pharma GmbH** (renamed from "AG"
  — resolves the in-document AG/GmbH inconsistency noted in baseline, since the exhibit
  itself flipped to GmbH); plus three NEW entities: Halozyme Hypercon, Inc. (DE), Elektrofi
  Security Corp. (Massachusetts, subsidiary of Hypercon), Halozyme Surf Bio, Inc. (DE).
- Delta: the FY2025 exhibit is the first to reflect the Elektrofi/Surf Bio deal
  structure, and the first to use "Antares Pharma GmbH" instead of "AG" — the FY2023/FY2024
  exhibits both say "AG," so the GmbH label is a FY2025-only change (contradicts the
  baseline's framing of this as a same-document inconsistency; it's actually a year-over-
  year rename that the main 10-K body's Note 2 text picked up early while the exhibit
  lagged, or vice versa).

## 4. Litigation (Merck/Keytruda)

- **FY2023 10-K**: 0 hits for "Merck" or "Keytruda." Item 3 Legal Proceedings is pure
  boilerplate: "We currently are not a party to any legal proceedings..."
- **FY2024 10-K**: identical — 0 hits, identical boilerplate Item 3 text.
- Confirms baseline: the Halozyme v. Merck suit (Apr 2025, D.N.J.) postdates both older
  filings and is absent from each, as expected.

## 5. Officer roster changes

- **FY2023 10-K executive officers**: Helen I. Torley (President/CEO/Director); Nicole
  LaBrosse (SVP & CFO, since Feb 2022); Mark Snyder (SVP, GC, Chief Compliance Officer &
  Secretary, since Jan 2022); Michael J. LaBarre (SVP, Chief Technical Officer, since Jan
  2020). **No COO listed.**
- **FY2024 10-K executive officers**: same "Executive Officers" section structure (Torley

  Cortney Caudill (COO since Oct 2025 per baseline) is absent, consistent with her
  appointment postdating the Feb 2025 filing.
- LaBrosse (CFO) present and unchanged in both older filings — her Nov 2025 Transition &
  Release Agreement (implying departure) is a FY2025-only event.
