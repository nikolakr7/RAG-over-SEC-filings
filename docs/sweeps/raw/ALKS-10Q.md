### Dated events (all POSTDATE 10-K)
- **April 1, 2026**: initiation of the **Brilliance Studies**, phase 3 program of alixorexton in adults with NT1 and NT2 (line 6427).

- Alixorexton regulatory designations now include FDA **Orphan Drug Designation for IH** and **European Commission ODD for narcolepsy** (line 6427); baseline 10-K listed only the Dec 2025 FDA Breakthrough Therapy designation for NT1.

### Litigation developments

## Q3 2025 (period ended September 30, 2025; filed 2025-10-28)

Source: `10-Q_2025-10-28_000119312525252524.txt`. The Avadel deal-announcement quarter; key delta vs baseline is the ORIGINAL deal economics, later improved.

### Avadel transaction (Subsequent Event, Note 17)
- **Oct 22, 2025** Transaction Agreement: **$18.50 per Avadel Share cash + $1.50 non-transferable CVR, total potential $20.00/share** (lines 5889-5899). The baseline 10-K's $21.00 cash reflects the Nov 18, 2025 Amendment No. 1, i.e. the amendment raised cash consideration by $2.50/share (amendment referenced at Q2 2026 file line 8225, 8-K filed Nov 19, 2025).
- Structure: court-sanctioned **scheme of arrangement under Irish law**; conditions include Avadel shareholder approval, Irish High Court sanction, and U.S. antitrust clearance, per Rule 2.7 announcement under the Irish Takeover Rules; expected close Q1 2026; expected Q4 2025 deal costs $15.0M-$20.0M (lines 5899-5908, 5934).
- **Bridge Credit Agreement, Oct 22, 2025**: JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. as Administrative Agent, Sole Lead Arranger and Sole Bookrunner; senior secured bridge facility up to **~$1.2B**, 364-day maturity from funding; plus **~$700.0M of cash placed into escrow** (lines 5910-5914, 7461). (Baseline shows the escrow at $731.2M by 12/31/2025 and the bridge amended and restated Nov 18, 2025.)

### Litigation (precise dates beyond baseline)
- **LYBALVI ANDA filing dates**: Teva sued **Aug 15, 2025** (NJ); complaint against Teva **amended Aug 20, 2025 to add an additional patent**; Apotex sued **Aug 27, 2025** (NJ) and **Aug 28, 2025** (DE); MSN sued **Sep 5, 2025** (NJ). Patents-in-suit expire between 2032 and 2041; 30-month stays triggered (line 5860).
- **Value Drug Company** antitrust class action filed **Oct 2, 2025** (D. Mass.), putative class of direct purchasers of VIVITROL, alleging U.S. Patent No. 7,919,499 fraudulently obtained, improperly Orange Book-listed and wrongfully enforced (line 5862).
- INVEGA TRINZA ANDA (Janssen v. Mylan): Mylan's petition for rehearing **denied July 1, 2025** (line 5855; Alkermes not a party).
- Acorda: Federal Circuit transferred the appeal to the Second Circuit on **July 25, 2025** (line 5877).
- Government matters here: "a subpoena and civil investigative demands from U.S. state and federal governmental authorities" (VIVITROL no longer specified) (line 5870).

### Agreements
- **Authorized Generic Product Supply Agreement, Sep 9, 2025, APIL and Amneal Pharmaceuticals LLC**, filed as exhibit to this 10-Q (line 7715). Matches baseline's Amneal AG Agreement.

## Q2 2025 (period ended June 30, 2025; filed 2025-07-29)

Source: `10-Q_2025-07-29_000095017025099409.txt`.

### New vs baseline: Zydus VUMERITY settlement (Biogen co-plaintiff; not in the 10-K)
- **VUMERITY ANDA litigation**: in July 2023, **Biogen Inc., Biogen Swiss Manufacturing GmbH and APIL** had sued **Zydus Worldwide DMCC, Zydus Pharmaceuticals (USA) Inc. and Zydus Lifesciences Limited** (D. Del.) over a generic of VUMERITY (diroximel fumarate) 231 mg, asserting U.S. Patent Nos. 8,669,281; 9,090,558; 10,080,733. **On July 21, 2025, the parties entered into a confidential settlement agreement** granting Zydus a license under the VUMERITY patents to launch a generic in the U.S. "beginning on an agreed date immediately prior to expiration of the latest to expire of the VUMERITY patents, or earlier under certain customary circumstances"; the DE District Court dismissed the case by stipulation (line 5422). This entire matter (and its settlement) is absent from the FY2025 10-K baseline.
- **Fountain Healthcare Partners Fund IV, L.P.**: in **February 2025** the Company committed up to **€10.0M** (~9.2% of total fund) to this healthcare/pharma/life-sciences VC partnership; equity method; $0.6M funded as of June 30, 2025 (lines 4330-4345). Not in baseline sweep.

### Other developments
- **July 2025**: alixorexton achieved the primary endpoint in **Vibrance-1** (NT1); phase 3 narcolepsy program planning announced (line 5625).
- INVEGA TRINZA appeal: Mylan filed petition for panel rehearing / rehearing en banc in **May 2025** (denied July 1, 2025 per Q3) (line 5418).
- Acorda: Federal Circuit transferred appeal to Second Circuit **July 25, 2025** (line 5431).
- Government matters: "a subpoena and civil investigative demands from U.S. state and federal governmental authorities **for documents related to VIVITROL**" (line 5424), the most specific wording in the series (later filings drop the VIVITROL tie).
- Novo Nordisk Athlone subcontracting continuing through end of 2025 (line 3073). No LYBALVI ANDA notices disclosed yet in this filing (Teva suit came Aug 15, 2025).
