## 8-K EVENTS (chronological)

1. **2026-04-29 (Item 2.02, earnings)**: Q1 2026 financial results press release. Earnings only.

2. **2026-05-28 (Items 7.01 + 8.01, partner clinical data)**: Ionis announced that partner **GSK** reported positive pivotal Phase 3 data (B-Well 1 and B-Well 2) for **bepirovirsen** (ASO for chronic hepatitis B), published in NEJM and presented at EASL. 19% functional cure rate overall (26% in the ≤1000 IU/mL HBsAg subgroup) vs 0% placebo. Bepirovirsen is under FDA priority review with Breakthrough and Fast Track designations; also under review in Europe, Japan (SENKU designation) and China; GSK anticipates first regulatory decisions Q3 2026. Filing restates deal terms: "GSK licensed bepirovirsen from Ionis in 2019 under a collaborative development and licensing agreement... tiered royalties of 10-12% on net sales."

3. **2026-06-04 event, filed 2026-06-08 (Items 5.02 + 5.07 + 7.01, board changes + annual meeting)**:
   - **Ludwig N. Hantson appointed to the Ionis Board** effective June 4, 2026 (immediately after the 2026 Annual Meeting). Bio from the 8-K: "He served as chief executive officer and board member of **Alexion** from 2017-2021, prior to its acquisition by **AstraZeneca**. Before joining Alexion, Dr. Hantson served as president of **Baxter Bioscience**, where he led the spin-off of **Baxalta**, serving as chief executive officer and board member. Earlier in his career, he held senior leadership roles at **Novartis** across North America and Europe, including chief executive officer of North America, and spent 13 years at **Johnson & Johnson**... In addition to serving as a director at Alexion and Baxalta, Dr. Hantson served as a director at **Hologic**." Ph.D. from University of Louvain, Belgium.
   - The filing also notes: "As previously announced in the Form 8-K filed with the SEC on March 9, 2026, the Company also appointed **Peter N. Reikes** as a member of the Company's Board effective June 4, 2026" (no bio in this filing; the March 9, 2026 8-K is not in this sweep's file set).
   - **Annual meeting votes (June 4, 2026)**: Berthelsen and Herman re-elected to terms ending 2029 (Berthelsen with a notably high 20.8M against votes); say-on-pay approved; 2011 Equity Incentive Plan share pool increased by 9.5M to 52M shares (32.3M against); ESPP amendment approved; **Ernst & Young LLP ratified as auditor for FY2026**. Parshall and Wender did not stand for re-election (board exits).

4. **2026-06-12 (Item 8.01, clinical data)**: Positive longer-term data from the CORE-OLE open-label extension of olezarsen (TRYNGOLZA) in sHTG presented at the National Lipid Association Scientific Sessions (Chicago): hepatic fat fraction (MRI-PDFF) returning toward baseline after 24 months on 80 mg; >90% patient retention in the OLE.

5. **2026-06-24 (Items 7.01 + 8.01, FDA approval)**: **FDA approved TRYNGOLZA (olezarsen) for severe hypertriglyceridemia (sHTG)**, its second indication (adjunct to diet to reduce triglycerides and acute pancreatitis risk; TG ≥500 mg/dL). Based on Phase 3 CORE and CORE2 (published in NEJM): up to 72% TG lowering vs placebo, up to 91% reduction in acute pancreatitis events. U.S. sHTG availability in July 2026. (This was the June 30, 2026 PDUFA catalyst flagged in the FY2025 10-K, approved early.)

6. **2026-07-09 (Items 7.01 + 8.01, Phase 3 failure)**: **CARDIO-TTRansform Phase 3 of eplontersen (WAINUA) in ATTR-CM FAILED its primary endpoint** (composite of CV mortality and recurrent CV events to Week 140 vs placebo). In the prespecified eplontersen-monotherapy subgroup a nominally significant HR 0.71 was seen; no treatment effect in patients on a baseline stabilizer (57% of each arm). "**Ionis and AstraZeneca** will continue to analyze the full data set," with results to be shared at the European Society of Cardiology Congress in August 2026. Major negative event for the AstraZeneca partnership (next milestones of $200M U.S. / $115M EU for ATTR-CM approval per the 10-K are now in doubt).

7. **2026-07-10, event dated 2026-07-09 (Item 7.01)**: Ionis reaffirmed its FY2026 financial guidance, unchanged from the April 29, 2026 Q1 release (damage-control filing the day after the CARDIO-TTRansform miss).

8. **2026-07-29 (Item 2.02, earnings)**: Q2 2026 financial results press release. Earnings only.

Signatory on all eight 8-Ks: **Patrick R. O'Neil**, EVP, Chief Legal Officer and General Counsel.
