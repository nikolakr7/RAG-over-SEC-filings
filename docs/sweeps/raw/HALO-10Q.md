## Item 1 Legal Proceedings — identical boilerplate in ALL SIX quarters
Every single 10-Q (2024-10-31 through 2026-08-06) carries the exact same generic Item 1
Legal Proceedings text: "We currently are not a party to any legal proceedings..." The
Halozyme v. Merck patent suit is **never** disclosed under Item 1 in any quarter — it only
ever appears in the MD&A "highlights" bullets and the Risk Factors section, never as a
formal Item 1 proceeding. Treat "Merck litigation" as a risk-factor/MD&A-sourced graph
edge, not an Item-1-sourced one.
