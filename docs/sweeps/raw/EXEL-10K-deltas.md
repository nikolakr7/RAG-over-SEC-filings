## 1. Deals

- **Merck (MSD International) zanzalintinib collaboration — FIRST APPEARS in FY2024 10-K.**
  FY2024 10-K L6154-6162: entered into Oct 2024, "clinical development collaboration with MSD
  International Business GmbH, known as Merck within the United States and Canada (Merck)" —
  zanza + KEYTRUDA (pembrolizumab) in STELLAR-305 (SCCHN, already ongoing); + WELIREG
  (belzutifan) in a phase 1/2 trial and two phase 3 pivotal RCC trials. Merck supplies KEYTRUDA;
  Merck sponsors/funds one Ph3 RCC study, Exelixis co-funds the phase 1/2 + other Ph3 and
  supplies zanza/cabo. Exelixis retains all zanza commercial/marketing rights. Accounted for
  under ASC Topic 808 (collaborative arrangement, not customer contract).
  **FY2023 10-K: zero hits for "MSD"/"Merck...collaboration"** — deal did not exist yet; only
  competitor-context mentions of pembrolizumab possible (not confirmed, not grepped further).
- **Zanza earlier development state (FY2024 10-K):** described only via Roche/atezolizumab, BMS
  nivolumab/ipilimumab/relatlimab, and (new) Merck's pembrolizumab/belzutifan combos — no
  Natera/Signatera (STELLAR-316, Jan 2026) mention, consistent with that deal postdating FY2024.
  STELLAR-311 (vs everolimus, NET) flagged as "anticipate will commence in first half of 2025" —
  i.e. not yet started as of FY2024 10-K. FY2023 10-K: STELLAR-304/305 combo partners are Roche
  atezolizumab + BMS nivolumab/ipilimumab/relatlimab + **Arcus' AB521** (Arcus absent from FY2024
  and FY2025 combo-partner lists — appears to have dropped out between FY2023 and FY2024).
- **2024 Restructuring Plan (new in FY2024 10-K, gone from baseline as a "current" item):**
  effective April 2024, terminated in-licensing deals with Aurigene Oncology Ltd., BioInvent
  International AB, Cybrexa Therapeutics LLC, NBE-Therapeutics AG, STORM Therapeutics LTD (none
  of these appear in the FY2025 baseline entity list — confirms they were fully wound down).
  Also in FY2024: discontinued GamaMabs Pharma SA IPR&D assets, and terminated the separate Nov
  2022 Catalent license (3 target programs) — this matches baseline's note of a Catalent deal
  "TERMINATED Dec 2024."
- **Teva settlement (Jul 2023) and Cipla settlement (May 2024)** are both present as completed
  litigation history in the FY2024 10-K but read as background, not new FY2024 deals — Teva
  license starts Jan 1, 2031; Cipla license starts Jan 1, 2031, both fully dismissed. Neither Teva
  nor Cipla appear in the FY2025 baseline's litigation section at all — confirms both are fully
  gone by FY2025 (settled and dismissed, non-recurring).

## 3. Litigation (generic cabozantinib) — progression across the two older 10-Ks

- **FY2023 10-K (filed 2024-02-06):** MSN II — bench trial occurred Oct 2023, "judgment expected
  first half of 2024" — i.e. **pre-ruling state**, no outcome yet. MSN I present but not detailed
  here. Teva: settled Jul 2023, dismissed Sep 2023 (already closed). Cipla: notice Feb 2023,
  complaint Mar 2023, stayed May 2023 (still open, pre-settlement — Cipla settles May 2024, after
  this filing). **No Sun Pharmaceutical, no Azurity, no Handa** anywhere in FY2023 10-K — none of
  these had yet sent notice letters.
- **FY2024 10-K (filed 2025-02-11):** MSN II ruling now present and fresh — Oct 15, 2024 Delaware
  District Court rejected MSN's invalidity challenges to '439/'440/'015; '349 patent held not
  invalid/not infringed; final judgment Oct 23, 2024 gates MSN ANDA approval to no earlier than
  **Jan 15, 2030**. MSN noticed CAFC appeal Nov 22, 2024; Exelixis cross-appealed Nov 26, 2024.
  New Feb 2025 MSN notice re: patent '039 (low impurity, expires 2032) — this is the seed of the
  "MSN '039" thread that matures later (baseline: complaint Mar 2025, stipulated infringement Aug
  2025). Cipla: settlement + dismissal now complete (May/Jul 2024). **Sun ANDA litigation first
  appears here**: notice letter Sep 17, 2024; Exelixis complaint filed Oct 30, 2024; Sun answered
  + counterclaimed Jan 22, 2025 (unresolved at filing — baseline shows Sun SETTLED Dec 2025,
  dismissed Dec 30, 2025, so this is mid-stream). **Azurity first appears here** too, but only as
  IPR petitioner (not yet a suit): petition re: '349 patent filed Nov 18, 2024 (filing date Dec
  12, 2024, response due Mar 12, 2025); second IPR petition re: '039 patent filed Jan 9, 2025 (no
  filing date yet). This predates baseline's Apr 2025 Azurity 505(b)(2) complaint and Jun/Jul 2025
  IPR denials. No Handa or Biocon mentions in either older 10-K.
