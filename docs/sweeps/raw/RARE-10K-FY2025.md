## ENTITIES

### The filer and aliases
- **Ultragenyx Pharmaceutical Inc.** = "Ultragenyx", "we/our/us", "the Company" (line 1816)

### Approved drugs (brand / generic / indication)
- **Crysvita (burosumab)** — anti-FGF23 monoclonal antibody for X-linked hypophosphatemia (XLH) and tumor-induced osteomalacia (TIO). Partnered with Kyowa Kirin. Approved US Apr 2018, EU/UK Feb 2018 (lines 1855-1858, 1965). Trademark licensed FROM KKC (line 2147). FY2025: $177M RARE-booked product sales (LatAm/Türkiye) + $304M royalty revenue; total Crysvita revenue incl. KKC-booked share $481M (lines 3538-3596, 8733-8737).
- **Mepsevii (vestronidase alfa)** — enzyme replacement (beta-glucuronidase) for mucopolysaccharidosis VII (MPS VII / Sly syndrome) (line 1861). In-licensed from Saint Louis University.
- **Dojolvi (triheptanoin)** — synthetic 7-carbon fatty acid triglyceride for long-chain fatty acid oxidation disorders (LC-FAOD); approved Jun 30, 2020 (lines 1863-1865, 3323). In-licensed from Baylor Research Institute.
- **Evkeeza (evinacumab)** — anti-ANGPTL3 monoclonal antibody for homozygous familial hypercholesterolemia (HoFH). Regeneron markets in US; Ultragenyx holds ex-US rights (EEA, Brazil, Mexico, Japan approved) (line 1869, 7160). Trademark licensed FROM Regeneron (line 2147).

### Clinical product candidates
- **UX111 (rebisufligene etisparvovec; formerly ABO-102)** — AAV9 gene therapy for Sanfilippo syndrome type A (MPS IIIA); licensed from Abeona Therapeutics May 2022 (line 1876). CRL from FDA July 2025 (CMC issues); BLA resubmitted Jan 2026; Incomplete Response Letter (IRL) received Feb 2026 (lines 1877-1879).
- **DTX401 (pariglasgene brecaparvovec)** — AAV8 gene therapy for glycogen storage disease type Ia (GSDIa); rolling BLA started Aug 2025, completed Dec 2025; PDUFA expected Q3 2026 (lines 1882-1886). From Dimension Therapeutics acquisition; NIH non-exclusive license on construct patent (line 2125).
- **GTX-102 (apazunersen)** — antisense oligonucleotide (ASO) for Angelman syndrome; via GeneTx acquisition; Phase 3 "Aspire" fully enrolled Jul 2025 (129 pts, data 2H 2026); Phase 2/3 "Aurora" enrollment began Oct 2025 (lines 1887-1903). Underlying patents licensed from Texas A&M University (line 2141).
- **DTX301 (avalotcagene ontaparvovec)** — AAV8 gene therapy for ornithine transcarbamylase (OTC) deficiency; Phase 3 "Enh3ance" enrollment completed Feb 2025 (lines 1906-1913). From Dimension; UPENN patent family sublicensed via REGENXBIO (line 2127).
- **UX701 (rivunatpagene miziparvovec)** — AAV9 gene therapy for Wilson disease; pivotal "Cyprus2+" Cohort 4 enrolled Sep 2025, Stage 1 data expected 2026 (lines 1916-1922). UPENN license (line 2144).
- **UX143 (setrusumab)** — anti-sclerostin monoclonal antibody for osteogenesis imperfecta (OI); with Mereo. Phase 3 "Orbit" and "Cosmic" FAILED primary endpoints, announced Dec 2025; program path under review (lines 1924-1939, 2591-2595).

## NOTABLE

### Five most concrete named risk factors
1. **UX143 (setrusumab) Phase 3 failure**: Orbit and Cosmic missed primary endpoints (Dec 2025), triggering a Feb 2026 strategic restructuring: ~10% workforce cut (~130 employees), ~$50M charges, UX143 CMO contract terminations accelerating ~$40M in payments (lines 2591-2595, 2383, 3107-3108, 4106, 10973-10990).
2. **Dependence on KKC** for Crysvita supply and commercialization in the US/Canada and Europe; KKC = 45% of total FY2025 revenue and sole burosumab manufacturer (lines 2448, 2373, 2165).
3. **Generic (ANDA) challenges to Dojolvi** by Navinta, Aurobindo, and Esjay; Hatch-Waxman suit pending in D.N.J.; orphan exclusivity ends 2027, FDA stay ends Dec 30, 2027 (lines 1949, 2933, 3322-3325).
4. **UX111 regulatory risk**: July 2025 CRL, Feb 2026 IRL after BLA resubmission; approval timing uncertain (lines 1784, 1877-1879).
5. **Intangible impairment / investment volatility**: $129M indefinite-lived IPR&D from the Dimension acquisition tied to DTX301/DTX401 outcomes; mark-to-market swings on Solid Biosciences stake (lines 3019, 3115, 8120-8123).

### Graph-interesting structural facts
- The Crysvita royalty stream has been sliced three ways to two different buyers (RPI 2019 Europe; OMERS 2022 US/Canada 30%; OMERS 2025 US/Canada +25% from 2028) with caps, an option to repurchase (1.35x, expires Nov 2027), and a subsidiary (Rare Delaware Inc.) as the selling entity — a textbook transferred-royalty subgraph (Note 10, lines 9168-9232).

- Ultragenyx sells Evkeeza everywhere except the U.S. while Regeneron sells it only in the U.S. — a clean territory-split edge on a single brand (lines 1869, 7160).
