## 8-K EVENTS (chronological)

1. **2026-02-24 (filed 2026-02-25, accession 000119312526068927) — Item 5.02 + 7.01: CEO succession.** Richard F. Pops notified the Board of his decision to retire as CEO effective **July 31, 2026**, after "a distinguished 35 years of service in this role." He will serve as **Senior Advisor through December 31, 2026** ($75,000/month cash retainer; pro-rated FY2026 bonus; pro-rated RSU award off a $2,687,500 grant value; 21-month post-service vesting protections) and remain on the Board as **non-executive Chairman** ($40,000 annual retainer, pro-rated). The Board appointed **Blair C. Jackson** (age 53, EVP/COO since January 2021; interim principal financial officer Feb 2024–Sep 2025; SVP Corporate Planning of Alkermes, Inc. Jul 2018–Jan 2021; degrees from University of Calgary, University of Alberta, MIT) as **CEO effective August 1, 2026**. Signed by David J. Gaffin, Secretary.

2. **2026-02-25 (accession 000119312526068512) — Item 2.02: earnings.** Q4 and FY2025 results announced plus FY2026 guidance; signed by CFO Joshua Reed. (Earnings only.)

3. **2026-05-05 (accession 000119312526205008) — Item 2.02: earnings.** Q1 2026 results and updated FY2026 guidance; signed by Joshua Reed. (Earnings only.)

4. **2026-05-12 (accession 000119312526218150) — Items 7.01/8.01: clinical data.** Alkermes announced **positive topline results from REVITALYZ**, a double-blind, placebo-controlled, randomized-withdrawal, multicenter **phase 3 study of LUMRYZ (sodium oxybate) extended-release oral suspension in adults with idiopathic hypersomnia** (investigational use). Signed by David J. Gaffin.

5. **2026-05-20 (accession 000119312526232584) — Items 5.02/5.07: annual meeting.** At the 2026 AGM shareholders (i) approved amendments to the **2018 Stock Option and Incentive Plan** adding 5,900,000 shares; (ii) elected all 9 directors for one-year terms to the 2027 AGM: Shane M. Cooke, Richard B. Gaynor, Cato T. Laurencin, Nancy S. Lurker, Brian P. McKeon, Richard F. Pops, Nancy L. Snyderman, Frank Anders Wilson, Christopher I. Wright; (iii) approved say-on-pay; (iv) ratified **PricewaterhouseCoopers LLP** as auditor; (v) renewed Irish-law share allotment and pre-emption disapplication authorities. Signed by David J. Gaffin.

6. **2026-06-15 (accession 000119312526270280) — Items 7.01/8.01: regulatory designations.** FDA granted **orphan drug designation to alixorexton for idiopathic hypersomnia**, and the **European Commission granted orphan drug designation to alixorexton for narcolepsy**. Signed by David J. Gaffin.

7. **2026-07-06 (filed 2026-07-08, accession 000119312526298004) — Item 1.02: termination of material agreement.** Alkermes and **Amneal Pharmaceuticals LLC** terminated the **Authorized Generic Product Supply Agreement dated September 9, 2025** (under which APIL had granted Amneal rights to distribute a limited quantity of an authorized generic VIVITROL for one year from a Third Party ANDA Product Launch). Alkermes notified Amneal it had not met certain terms; Amneal declined to order any Batches before the cure period expired. No penalties; mutual release of all claims. Signed by David J. Gaffin. (Kills the authorized-generic supply edge noted in the 10-K baseline.)

8. **2026-07-28 (accession 000119312526318844) — Item 2.02: earnings.** Q2/H1 2026 results and updated FY2026 guidance; signed by Joshua Reed. (Earnings only.)
