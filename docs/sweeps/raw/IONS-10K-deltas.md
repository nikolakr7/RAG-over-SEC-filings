## DEAL DELTAS

### AstraZeneca — WAINUA (eplontersen) joint dev/commercialization (2021)
- **FY2023** (~3633-3643, ~13520-13573): Total potential **$3.6B = $200M upfront + $20M license fee + $485M dev/approval milestones + $2.9B sales milestones**. Royalties: mid-20% U.S.; ex-U.S. **"ranging from mid to high teens"**. Scope history disclosed only here: Ionis **initially retained certain Latin American countries; in July 2023 expanded AZ's ex-U.S. rights to include Latin America** ($20M license fee, ~13558-13560). Cumulative received: **nearly $360M**. 2023 events: **$50M milestone when FDA approved WAINUA (Dec 2023)** (~13570-13573). Next milestone then: **$30M on EU ATTRv-PN approval** (~13543-13546). AZ pays 55% of global Phase 3 costs (~3637-3638).
- **FY2024** (~2889-2905, ~12319-12380): U.S. launch **January 2024** — royalty stream begins (~2901-2902). Ex-U.S. royalty language becomes **"up to the high teens"** (mid-teens floor language dropped, ~12334-12337). Q4 2024: **$30M milestone on MHRA approval of WAINZUA (UK)** (~12377-12380). Cumulative: **>$520M generated / >$465M received**. Next milestone shifts to **$200M (U.S. ATTR-CM approval) or $115M (Europe)** (~12341-12346) — same as FY2025 baseline; received grows to >$575M by FY2025.

### AstraZeneca — CVRM strategic collaboration (2015) — terms moved every year
- **FY2023** (~3649-3654, ~13583-13625): Total potential **$3.4B = $65M upfront + up to $290M license fees + $865M development milestones + $2.2B regulatory milestones** (no sales-milestone bucket). Royalties **"up to the low teens"**. Received **>$300M**; next payment **$10M** if AZ advances a medicine. 2023: **$36M payment when AZ licensed ION826** (~13621-13625).
- **FY2023 PNPLA3 program in healthy state** (~3430-3437): **ION839 (AZD2693)**, PNPLA3 LICA medicine for NASH, in an AZ-run **Phase 2b** in patients with NASH+fibrosis carrying the PNPLA3 mutation.
- **FY2024** (~2909-2915, ~12384-12411): **December 2024 amendment restructured economics**: total potential rises to **$4.3B = $65M upfront + $175M license fees + $514M dev + $863M regulatory + $2.8B sales milestones**; royalties cut to **"up to 10 percent"**. Received **>$340M** (>$370M generated); next payment up to $30M. **PNPLA3/ION839/AZD2693 has zero mentions anywhere in the FY2024 filing** — silently dropped from the pipeline a year before the formal AZ termination (Nov 2025, per baseline). By FY2025 the stated total potential is back down to **$3.4B** (post-PNPLA3-termination haircut of ~$0.9B).

### Biogen — 2013 Strategic Neurology collaboration (VANISHED after FY2023)
- **FY2023 only** (~3583-3603): Named "2013 Strategic Neurology" collaboration; Biogen exclusive antisense rights, options post-Phase-2 PoC; expanded 2016. Four active programs: **ION859 (Parkinson's), QALSODY + ION541 (ALS), ION464 (multiple system atrophy)**. QALSODY option exercised **December 2018** (~2623). Cumulative: **>$325M received**. QALSODY royalties 11-15%.
- **FY2024**: the collaboration name and **ION859, ION541, ION464 all have zero mentions** — the whole 2013 collaboration and its three remaining programs disappeared between the FY2023 and FY2024 10-Ks; FY2024/FY2025 group Biogen neurology as "2018 and 2012" only (~2959-2960 in FY2024 filing). QALSODY is thereafter presented standalone ("In 2018, Biogen exercised its option," ~2941).

### Biogen — 2012 neurology / ION582 (obudanersen) option — healthy state then expiry
- **FY2023** (~3609-3625, ~13410-13457): ION582 developed **under the 2012 neurology collaboration with option live**: next payment **$70M if Biogen licenses ION582** (~13431-13434); **$39M in milestones received during 2023 for advancing ION582** (~13421-13423); per-program economics: license fee + up to $10M dev + $130M regulatory milestones + mark-up on Phase 1/2 costs + royalties up to mid-teens (~13405-13415). Cumulative 2012-collab payments >$230M. HALOS Phase 1/2 completed enrollment Nov 2023 (~3226-3227).
- **FY2024** (~12691-12695): **"In the second quarter of 2024, Biogen's option to license ION582 ... expired unexercised"**; Ionis recognized **$30M of previously deferred milestone revenue**; total ION582 milestones included: $68M (~12686-12688). The $70M license fee never materialized; Ionis retains full rights (Phase 3 REVEAL planned, ~2673). Matches baseline's wholly owned obudanersen.
- SPINRAZA cumulative trajectory: FY2023 **>$2.0B total (> $1.6B royalties)** (~13546-13547) → FY2024 **>$2.3B (~$1.9B royalties)** (~2933-2934) → FY2025 >$2.5B (>$2.0B).
- Biogen aggregate (FY2023 only): **>$3.8B generated from all Biogen collaborations** (~3533). 2018 Strategic Neurology: "nearly $1.1B" by FY2023 (~3581); 10-year technology exclusivity from 2018 (~3574-3575).
- Biogen revenue concentration series: 2021 **53%**, 2022 **62%**, 2023 **44%** (FY2023 ~13483-13495), 2024 **52%** (FY2024 ~12722-12734), 2025 31% (baseline). Biogen deferred revenue: **$307.4M (12/31/2023)** (~13504-13505) → **$211.0M (12/31/2024)** (~12743-12744) → $151.2M (12/31/2025, baseline).

### Novartis — pelacarsen (2017): stagnant deal, full original terms
- **FY2023** (~13754-13788): Total potential **$900M = $75M upfront + $150M license fee + $25M development milestone + up to $290M regulatory + $360M sales milestones**; royalties mid-teens to low-20%; **Novartis exercised its option February 2019** (~2900-2901); companion **SPA: Novartis bought 1.6M Ionis shares for $100M in 2017** (~13783-13788). Received >$275M; next **$50M on FDA NDA acceptance**. Identical received/next figures in FY2024 (~2985-2993) and FY2025 — no payment motion for 3+ fiscal years while Lp(a)HORIZON runs.

### Novartis — next-generation Lp(a) collaboration (Aug 2023): first appearance → termination
- **FY2023 first appearance, original signing terms** (~3683-3689, ~13890-13928): Collaboration and license agreement **August 2023**; total potential **$730M = $60M upfront + up to $155M development + $105M regulatory + $410M sales milestones**; royalties **10-20%**; Novartis solely responsible for development/manufacturing/commercialization. Received $60M (upfront, Q4 2023); next **$5M on development-candidate designation**; Ionis research term expected to end **June 2024**; $30.0M Novartis deferred revenue at 12/31/2023 (~13968-13970).
- **FY2024** (~2995-3001, ~12903-12947): still active; received **>$65M**; R&D performance obligation completed **Q3 2024**; next $10M "if Novartis advances a medicine."
- **FY2025** (baseline): **terminated by Novartis in 2025** — Ionis realized ~$65M of the $730M potential.

### Roche — sefaxersen (IONIS-FB-LRx): GA indication decay
- **FY2023** (~3708-3731): Program still branded **IONIS-FB-LRx** (sefaxersen name not yet used); **two indications active: IgAN and GA (dry AMD)**; Ionis itself conducting/funding the Phase 2 GA study and open-label Phase 2 IgAN study; Roche licensed 2022; Phase 3 IgAN initiated April 2023; received **>$135M**. Positive Phase 2 IgAN interim data Oct 2023 (~2509).
- **FY2024** (~3005-3017): renamed sefaxersen; **July 2024: Roche discontinued the GA program** after Phase 2 (favorable safety, insufficient efficacy); received >$140M. Baseline adds IgAN-only cap ($430M) and next $23.5M.
- Roche Sept 2023 AD/HD agreement: first appearance FY2023 (~3735-3741, $60M upfront received, "generated $60 million"); FY2024 >$60M→"more than $60 million" (~3042-3043); the $50M Phase 1 milestone only lands Jan 2026 (baseline).

### Otsuka — one-market deal grows into multi-asset franchise
- **FY2023 first appearance** (~3745-3751): December 2023, **donidalorsen Europe only**; royalties **"20 to 30 percent"** on European net sales; **$65M generated** ($65M upfront, ~13233 area). Ulefnersen at this date is **wholly Ionis-owned** (no partner; Phase 3 initiated April 2021; formerly ION363, ~2933-2945).
- **FY2024** (~3047-3057): expanded — **Asia-Pacific rights added Q2 2024**; **November 2024 worldwide ulefnersen commercialization agreement**; royalties restated "up to 30 percent"; cumulative **>$110M** (incl. $15M EMA MAA-acceptance milestone 2024, per baseline). FY2025: >$120-125M.

### Sobi / PTC / Theratechnologies / Ono
- **Sobi TEGSEDI North America termination — earliest state**: FY2023 (~2423-2424, ~14314-14315): terminated **October 2023**, Sobi "transitioning responsibilities to us," and — detail absent from baseline — **in February 2024 Ionis began withdrawing the TEGSEDI NDA** in the U.S. FY2024 (~3070): TEGSEDI NA discontinued in 2024; Sobi cumulative >$60M. **Sobi TRYNGOLZA ex-U.S. deal (March 2025) absent from both older filings** (FY2024 10-K filed 2025-02-19, pre-deal; no subsequent-events note).
- **Ono Pharmaceutical: zero mentions in either older filing** — the $280M-upfront sapablursen license (March 10, 2025) postdates the FY2024 filing date; first appearance is FY2025. Ono jumped from nonexistent to 30% of revenue in one filing cycle.
- **PTC**: FY2023 gives no cumulative total (~3753-3757); FY2024 **>$55M** (~3061-3064); FY2025 >$70M.
- **Theratechnologies (Canada, donidalorsen + olezarsen)**: first appearance FY2024 (December 2024 agreement, ~3072-3075); absent FY2023.
- **Royalty Pharma**: first appearance FY2023 (January 2023 royalty purchase agreement; $500M upfront + up to $625M milestones; 25% of future pelacarsen royalties, ~13882-13884, ~13150); terms stable across all three filings.
- **Dating inconsistency (Bicycle)**: FY2023 filing says the Bicycle collaboration began **"In 2021"** (~3497); FY2024 (~3085-3086) and FY2025 filings say **"In 2020"** with 2021 option exercise.
