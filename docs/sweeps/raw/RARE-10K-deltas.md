# RARE — 10-K deltas vs FY2025 baseline (older 10-Ks: FY2024 filed 2025-02-19, FY2023 filed 2024-02-21)

Baseline: `docs/sweeps/raw/RARE-10K-FY2025.md`. Sources: `data/text/RARE/10-K_2025-02-19_000095017025023312.txt` (+ EX21), `data/text/RARE/10-K_2024-02-21_000095017024017879.txt` (+ EX21). Line numbers refer to each respective file.

## 1. Deals — deltas

### REGENXBIO ("REGENX") — ACTIVE in both older filings (baseline: both terminated Nov 2025)
Both older 10-Ks describe two live agreements, unchanged wording across FY2023 and FY2024 filings (2025-02-19 lines 2225-2235; 2024-02-21 ~lines 2244-2256):
- **Oct 2013 exclusive license** (option-based): OTC deficiency + GSDIa. Annual maintenance fee, per-indication milestone fees, low-to-mid single-digit royalties, pass-through of REGENX's own royalty/sublicense obligations to its licensors. Milestone-deadline extension mechanism (pay REGENX to extend).
- **Mar 2015 option and license agreement** (amended): Wilson disease + CDKL5 deficiency. Mid-to-high single-digit royalties, mid-single-to-low-double-digit % of any sublicense fees received.
- **Mar 2020 license**: NAV AAV8/AAV9 vectors for a rare metabolic disorder gene therapy. Upfront payment + annual fees/milestones/royalties (high-single to low-double-digit).
No termination language anywhere in either older filing — confirms both deals were still active through FY2024 (filed Feb 2025); termination is a FY2025-only (Nov 2025) event.

### Crysvita royalty ladder — earlier states (baseline: RPI 2019 + OMERS 2022 + OMERS 2025 three-way split)
Both older filings show only **two** royalty sales (RPI 2019 + OMERS 2022) — the 2025 OMERS tranche does not exist yet:
- RPI/Royalty Pharma: Dec 17, 2019 sale of European Territory royalty, effective Jan 1, 2020, $320M (unchanged terms both years; 2025-02-19 line 4178).
- OMERS: Jul 14, 2022 sale of 30% of US/Canada royalty, $500M, capped at $725M (2025-02-19 lines 4200-4206; 2024-02-21 lines 4181-4189).
- Effective interest rates given: FY2024 10-K states 6.2% (RPI) / 7.5% (OMERS) as of Dec 31, 2024 (2025-02-19 line 4206) — both **higher** than baseline FY2025 rates (5.5% RPI / 7.3% OMERS-2022), i.e. rates drifted down as the arrangements amortized further.
- KKC US-promotion-rights sunset date **moved twice**: FY2023 10-K (2024-02-21 line 9612) says Ultragenyx's cost-share commercial support in the U.S. extended "through December 31, 2024" (after a 2022 amendment originally through April 2024); FY2024 10-K (2025-02-19 line 9922) says extended further "through January 31, 2025" — matches baseline's Jan 31, 2025 cutoff, so that date was fixed by the FY2024 10-K, not present in FY2023 10-K.

### Mereo / setrusumab — pre-failure state (baseline: Phase 3 Orbit/Cosmic failed Dec 2025)
FY2024 10-K (2025-02-19 lines 2037-2073, 2251-2257) describes setrusumab as an active, promising Phase 3 program: Orbit (159 pts) and Cosmic (69 pts) enrolling/underway; positive interim data (May 24, 2024 cut-off) showing "statistically significant reductions in fractures" and BMD improvements; safety profile clean (no hypersensitivity reactions). Same deal terms as baseline ($50M upfront, $9M milestones paid, up to $245M future, tiered double-digit royalties both directions) plus the Dec 2024 manufacturing/supply agreement (new in the FY2024 10-K; absent from FY2023 10-K). No hint of trial risk beyond generic risk-factor language — confirms failure was unforeseen as of Feb 2025 filing.

### Kyowa Kirin (KKC) terms — consistent, with the promotion-date evolution noted above
Collaboration structure (Profit-Share Territory US/Canada, European Territory, LatAm/Turkey) and 30%-of-net-sales KKC transfer price for LatAm unchanged across all three years. No amendment-count language comparison possible (older filings don't total "13+ amendments" the way baseline's context suggests).
