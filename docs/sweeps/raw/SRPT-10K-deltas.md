# SRPT 10-K deltas: FY2024 (filed 2025-02-28) and FY2023 (filed 2024-02-28) vs FY2025 baseline

Sources: `data/text/SRPT/10-K_2025-02-28_000095017025029973.txt` ("FY2024 10-K") + its EX21; `data/text/SRPT/10-K_2024-02-28_000095017024022036.txt` ("FY2023 10-K") + its EX21. Line numbers refer to each respective file.

## 1. Deals

### Arrowhead — introduced fresh in FY2024 10-K; ABSENT from FY2023 10-K
- FY2023 10-K: zero mentions of Arrowhead (deal postdates it; signed Nov 25, 2024, after FY2023 period).
- FY2024 10-K (lines 2389-2429): full first telling of the "Arrowhead Collaboration Agreement," dated Nov 25, 2024. Same structure as baseline: 4 clinical programs (DUX4/FSHD, DMPK/DM1, ATXN2/ataxias, MMP7/IPF) + 3 pre-clinical (ATXN1, ATXN3, HTT) + 6 discovery targets. Terms match baseline: $500.0M upfront cash (paid at closing Feb 7, 2025), up to $300.0M DM1 near-term payments, up to $250.0M annual fees, $110-180M dev milestones/program, $500-700M sales milestones/program, tiered royalties up to low double digits. Equity: Sarepta Therapeutics Investments, Inc. bought 11,926,301 Arrowhead shares for $325.0M on Feb 7, 2025 (line 2429).
- FY2024 10-K financial notes (line 10041 area): "For the year ended December 31, 2024, there was no accounting impact as a result of the execution of the Arrowhead Agreement because the closing of the transaction did not occur until subsequent to year-end" (line 10074) — deal signed in FY2024 but economically a FY2025 event; the $500M upfront + related R&D expense hit Q1 2025 (line 10047-10052).

### Roche ex-US ELEVIDYS option exercises, per year
- FY2024 10-K (line 10105): "On February 12, 2024, Roche declined to exercise a certain Option related to one external, early-stage development program, which resulted in that Option's expiry and the immediate recognition of the value assigned to that Option as collaboration revenue" — this is the $48.0M 2024 revenue event cited in baseline. Confirms baseline's chronology; FY2024 10-K is the filing that actually books it.
- FY2023 10-K: no Feb 2024 option-decline event (postdates FY2023 year-end); general Roche option language present (lines 2174-2206) but no exercise/decline history yet recounted.
- Neither older 10-K yet mentions the Feb 2025 or Feb 2026 option expiries (both postdate their filings).

### BioMarin royalties, pre-expiry state (both older 10-Ks say materially the same thing, still live at each filing date)
- Both FY2024 10-K (lines 2325-2329) and FY2023 10-K (lines 2226-2236) describe royalties as **not yet expired**: 4-8% by geography through June 30, 2022, then 4% U.S./5% ex-US per the 2021 Amendment, with royalty term "will expire" (FY2023 wording, line 2228, future tense) vs "expired" (FY2024 wording, line 2327, past tense) on March 31, 2024 (U.S.) / Dec 31, 2024 (ex-US). This is the clearest tense-based temporal marker: FY2023 10-K written before the U.S. expiry date; FY2024 10-K written after both expiries had passed, narrating them retrospectively but the License Agreement's own "expired" status (baseline's full expiry language) is stated identically once both dates pass.
- MD&A confirms live royalty cost impact: FY2023 10-K line 5329 attributes part of a cost-of-sales decrease to "a decrease in royalty payments due to changes in the BioMarin royalty terms" (the July 2022 4%/5% step-down actually reducing expense in-year).

## 2. Exhibit 21 changes
- **FY2023 10-K EX21 (filed 2024-02-28) — only 4 subsidiaries listed**: Sarepta Securities Corp. (MA), ST International Holdings Two, Inc. (DE), Sarepta Therapeutics Three, LLC (DE). Notably MISSING relative to later years: ST International Holdings, Inc. (the "One," non-"Two" entity), Sarepta Therapeutics Two, LLC, Sarepta Therapeutics Ireland LLP, Sarepta Therapeutics Ireland Two LLP.
- **FY2024 10-K EX21 (filed 2025-02-28) — full 7-subsidiary list**, identical to the FY2025 baseline: Sarepta Securities Corp., ST International Holdings, Inc., ST International Holdings Two, Inc., Sarepta Therapeutics Two, LLC, Sarepta Therapeutics Three, LLC, Sarepta Therapeutics Ireland LLP, Sarepta Therapeutics Ireland Two LLP.
- Delta: the Ireland entities and "ST International Holdings, Inc." / "Sarepta Therapeutics Two, LLC" were added to the Exhibit 21 list sometime between the FY2023 and FY2024 filings (i.e., during calendar 2024).
- **Sarepta Therapeutics Investments, Inc.** (the Arrowhead equity-purchase vehicle) is absent from all three years' Exhibit 21 lists — confirms the baseline's noted oddity is not year-specific; it has never appeared in Exhibit 21 despite being a real, named, wholly-owned subsidiary described in prose.

## 3. Litigation, earlier states

### Nippon Shinyaku (D. Del., filed July 13, 2021)
- FY2023 10-K (line 2609-2610): case still at the pleading/characterization stage — "Nippon Shinyaku is seeking a determination of invalidity and Sarepta is seeking counterclaims of infringement." No mention yet of IPRs, PTAB, jury trial, or verdict (those all happened later in 2022-2024).
- FY2024 10-K (lines 14599-14603): full history through the Dec 2024 jury verdict — NS's '092 patent invalid, Sarepta/UWA's '851 patent valid and infringed, $115.2M damages (through Dec 15, 2024) + $0.8M stipulated ex-US royalty damages, judgment entered Jan 7, 2025, post-trial motions pending as of the Feb 28, 2025 filing date. This matches baseline's #12 litigation summary almost exactly (baseline is describing the same litigation one year further along).
- Japan companion case (Sarepta v. NS, filed June 5, 2023, Japan): FY2023 10-K describes it as pending with a "final hearing set for July 11, 2024" (last line of file's Nippon section); FY2024 10-K reports Sarepta abandoned its claims and the case terminated Jan 30, 2025 — a Sarepta-initiated case Sarepta itself walked away from.

### Regenxbio / U-Penn ('617 Patent case, filed Sept 15, 2020)
- FY2023 10-K and FY2024 10-K tell an identical story through the same milestone (both files contain the same paragraph almost verbatim, lines ~14357/14588): summary judgment for Sarepta Jan 5, 2024 (patent-ineligible under Section 101), judgment entered and case closed Jan 12, 2024, Regenx/U-Penn appealed to the Federal Circuit. (Baseline reports the Federal Circuit later REVERSED Feb 20, 2026 — postdates both older 10-Ks.)
- Second Regenx/U-Penn case ('274 Patent, filed June 20, 2023, vs Sarepta + Catalent): FY2023 10-K (filed Feb 2024) shows it freshly answered (Aug 10, 2023) with a trial date then set for Nov 17, 2025. FY2024 10-K (filed Feb 2025) adds the IPR petition (filed Feb 21, 2024) and PTAB institution of inter partes review (Aug 22, 2024) — the case moved from litigation-only to a parallel PTAB track between the two filings.
- Genzyme v. Sarepta ('542/'721 patents): absent from FY2023 10-K (filed before the July 26, 2024 filing date). First appears in FY2024 10-K (line 14607) with trial then scheduled for Jan 25, 2027 (baseline's later filing shows the trial date moved to June 14, 2027 and 5 more patents added in May 2025 — both postdate the FY2024 10-K).

## 4. ELEVIDYS's rise before the 2025 safety crisis — optimistic-era language
- FY2023 10-K (filed Feb 28, 2024, line 2027): "Even though EXONDYS 51, VYONDYS 53, AMONDYS 45 and ELEVIDYS **(with respect to non-ambulatory patients)** have received accelerated approval from the FDA..." — the parenthetical narrows ELEVIDYS's accelerated-approval status specifically to non-ambulatory patients as of this filing (reflecting that only the June 2023 ambulatory 4-5 accelerated approval, not yet a broader label, existed at the time; full/traditional ambulatory approval and non-ambulatory accelerated approval both landed June 2024, after this 10-K).
- FY2024 10-K (filed Feb 28, 2025, line 3342): identical sentence but the "(with respect to non-ambulatory patients)" qualifier is DROPPED — by this filing ELEVIDYS's approval status had broadened (June 2024 traditional approval for ambulatory + accelerated for non-ambulatory), so the caveat was no longer needed. Neither file contains any boxed-warning, death, or shipment-suspension language (all 2025 events).
- Geographic rollout as of each filing (temporal contrast, both matter-of-fact/optimistic, no safety caveats): FY2023 10-K (line 2861) — "VYONDYS 53 and ELEVIDYS have only been approved for sale and marketing in the U.S." FY2024 10-K (line 2996) — ELEVIDYS "approval for sale and marketing... in the U.S., and our strategic partner Roche has received approvals in certain other countries" (ex-US rollout beginning via Roche, pre-Japan-approval).
- Data/orphan exclusivity framing identical and stable across both older 10-Ks (line 2360/2515): "In connection with its FDA approval on June 22, 2023, the FDA granted ELEVIDYS... data exclusivity until June 22, 2035, and Orphan Drug Exclusivity until June 22, 2030" — no mention of the later AAVrh74 platform-designation grant/revocation cycle (2025-only event).
